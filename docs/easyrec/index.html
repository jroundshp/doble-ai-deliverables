The 3D viewer is not a plugin

The Open Channel Flow flume page you liked runs model-viewer, a web component Google publishes and gives away. There is nothing to install, nothing to license and no page builder involved. It reads the same GLB that Conrad already exports from SolidWorks.

Here it is running on this page. Drag it. On a phone, use one finger.

Live, right now

That is a sample model from the project's own documentation, used here so you can see the behaviour before you have a bench file. Yours would be Conrad's GLB.

To use it: upload the GLB to the media library like any other file, copy its URL, and drop this into an Elementor HTML widget.

<script type="module"
  src="https://ajax.googleapis.com/ajax/libs/model-viewer/4.0.0/model-viewer.min.js"></script>

<model-viewer
  src="https://easyrecreation.com/wp-content/uploads/YOUR-FILE.glb"
  alt="Easy Stack Bench"
  camera-controls
  auto-rotate
  style="width:100%;height:480px">
</model-viewer>

One thing to watch. Keep the GLB small. Anything over about 10 MB is slow on a phone, and SolidWorks exports are heavier than they need to be by default. If a file feels sluggish, ask Conrad to decimate the mesh before export.

Pick a color, drop a logo on it

This is the piece you were hunting for a plugin to do. It is a single block of HTML with no dependencies. Try it here first: choose a color, upload any logo file from your computer, then drag it and size it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. The file never leaves your browser.

The three hidden fields at the bottom of the code carry the color, the file name and the placement, so your quote form collects all three with the request. Your rep stops reading "see notes" and calling to ask.

It needs one photograph from you. The sixteen color files on the bench page are flat illustrations of the bench, 209 by 143 pixels. They were made as swatches for the color grid, which is all that page asks of them, and there is nothing larger on the server. The preview above is capped at a size where they still hold up, but they will not carry a full-width feature.

What fixes it is one photograph: a plain white bench, no graphics on it, shot square-on from the side. All sixteen colors come off that single image, and a customer's logo then sits on the real surface instead of on a drawing. There is a switch at the top of the code (USE_PHOTO) for exactly that. Your IMG_3806 is the right shot, but the InterActive Play logo is already printed on the panel, so it cannot host somebody else's.

Why not 3D for this one. Putting a customer's artwork on a 3D bench means Conrad rebuilds each model with a surface reserved and mapped for the graphic, and again every time a product changes. The flat version does the job the customer actually wants done, which is seeing their own logo on their own color before they commit.

Why the measuring form generates phone calls

The reps are right not to trust what comes back. The form carries the instructions but not the drawings, and that guide is almost entirely drawings. Four specific things, all of them structural rather than anybody's fault:

  1. The first water depth reading has no label of its own. Points two, three and four are each named. Point one shares its box with the paragraph of instructions above it, so that number lands in the spreadsheet with nothing to say what it measures.
  2. No question asks which gutter profile the pool has. A through F are described, then six open boxes appear and the customer picks one to type into with no picture in front of them.
  3. Multi-point steps arrive as one line of text. The rounded-corner step asks for seven measurements typed into a single box, in order, separated by commas.
  4. The photos cannot be attached. They are required, but they arrive as a separate email that somebody matches back to the right row by hand.

Every one of those is a phone call. The PDF alternative is a 15 MB download, which is its own problem at the side of a pool.

What belongs there instead

One step per screen on a phone, with the drawing on screen and a box beside each circle on it, so the customer fills in the picture rather than translating it into a sentence.

Tap your gutter profile and only those boxes appear. The photo uploads inside the same submission. Anything outside the ranges you already publish, the 30 to 58 inch width and the 36 to 58 inch depth, gets caught while they are still at the pool with the tape in their hand.

Your rep opens one labeled summary per pool.

That is a build rather than a paste, which is why it is the piece worth handing over. Send a login for the new site and it gets built there, then you review it and change whatever you want changed.

Worth a minute today

The new site is open to Google

Unrelated to any of the above. new.easyrecreation.com is being crawled as it stands. All eight pages load normally, none carry a noindex tag, robots.txt invites crawling and the sitemap is live, so the sample page and the default hosting post are public under the title "My WordPress – Blank".

  1. In the new site's WordPress admin, go to Settings → Reading.
  2. Tick Discourage search engines from indexing this site, and save.
  3. Untick it the day you launch, and not before.

It is not a permanent fix and it is not meant to be. It is the thirty-second version that stops a half-built site from being indexed under someone else's name for your products.
