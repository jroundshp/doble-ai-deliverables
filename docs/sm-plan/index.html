05 · The seven channels

Same message, same week, everywhere a guest looks

Each property has its own objectives and its own plan. Within each plan, all seven channels move together.

Website

Each month's offer goes on the site the week it starts: holiday party sections, order-by dates, gift card links, holiday hours. Doble AI leads the changes with each team.

Google Business Profile

For many guests this is the first and only thing they see. Hours match the site, holiday hours are set ahead, one update a week goes out, new photos are added monthly and every review gets an answer.

Email

Alpine's list goes out about twice a month, with a Bake Shop block in every send. Fenimore's starts collecting from Nov 1 toward a first 500 addresses, and the Clubhouse Teams form adds an opt-in.

Social media

Facebook and Instagram on a weekly rhythm, plus dated posts for every holiday, event and big game. Key posts are scheduled from one hub a month ahead.

Printed materials and QR codes

Signs, table tents, counter cards, flyers and the Huntley House brochure carry the month's message. Every QR code goes straight to the page that books or buys, never just the home page. Every piece has a date to go up and a date to come down.

TV screens

Any screen in the room is a sign that changes itself. When there's no game on, the screens run a loop of the month's message: the specials, holiday parties, events and catering, gift cards, and a QR code to book or sign up. Fenimore's has nine. Where Alpine, the Bake Shop or Huntley House have a screen, it runs the same loop. The slides are built from the hub each month with the posts.

Staff

Everyone knows their own property and its sister properties: what each sells and when each is open. A 20-minute kickoff the week of Oct 26, a 10-minute refresh each month, and a one-page card at every register.

14 · An open question

Ellicottville Coffee Company and Alpine Bistro: how do they work together?

What's the main brand? How do the two names work together? And what's the best way to reach a final answer we all stick to? Today the Ellicottville Coffee name shows up in several places that don't connect, and there's no single program behind it.

Where the name lives today

  • ellicottvillecoffee.com, the online store for beans and gift cards
  • Its own Google Business Profile
  • An @ellicottvillecoffee Instagram account that hasn't posted in a while
  • An Ellicottville Coffee page on the Alpine Bistro site
  • The whole-bean bags, next to other roasters' bags on the shelf
  • Soon, the Ellicottville Coffee Martini on the specialty menu

The usual answers

  1. Alpine Bistro is the main brand, and Ellicottville Coffee is its coffee: the beans, the coffee drinks, the coffee cocktails.
  2. Ellicottville Coffee Company is the main brand, and Alpine Bistro is the café and bar where it's served.
  3. Two separate brands, each with its own program, accounts and budget.

Each one changes the signs, the website, the Google profiles, the social accounts and the bags, so it's worth settling once.

A way to decide
