The Core Gap — The Firm Rebranded. The Machine-Readable Website Did Not.

Start with the good news, because there is a lot of it. The hosting is professional grade (WP Engine behind Cloudflare, a 60ms server response, valid SSL, a correct redirect from the bare domain), the mobile viewport does not block pinch-to-zoom, which is the defect on every other site in the portfolio, and the content library is enormous: 688 articles, 103 practice pages, 108 FAQ pages and 23 attorney bios. There is no case for a rebuild here.

The problem is that the November 2022 rebrand was completed for humans and never completed for machines. Nearly four years on, 39 of the 40 blog posts we sampled still carry "HoganWillig Law Firm" in the page title tag, which is the single line Google and every AI assistant read first. The site's Organization markup still lists the old firm's Facebook and X accounts as its official profiles, and the X account is dead. The robots.txt file still points every crawler at a sitemap on hoganwillig.com that returns a 404. And on the old domain itself, only the homepage redirects: every deep link from fifty years of citations, directory listings and press coverage lands on a 404.

The result is a firm that reads to a search engine as two half-entities instead of one strong one. Ask an AI who handles residential closings in Buffalo, and it names Clark Peshkin, Smolarek Law and LoTempio. Tiveron, which by its own account does more residential closings than any other real estate group in the Buffalo area, is not in the answer. None of this is construction. It is naming, markup and redirects.

03 Where They Stand

Tiveron has the two things that are genuinely hard to acquire: scale and reputation. Five offices, 35+ attorneys, nearly a thousand published pages and 191 reviews at 4.2 stars. What it lacks are the things that cost the least: a consistent machine-readable name, working redirects from its own history, and content labelled so an assistant can quote it.

Real Strengths
Professional hosting and a fast, secure, mobile-correct site. A content library most firms would envy. 191 reviews at 4.2 stars on the main profile. Genuine third-party recognition from Super Lawyers, Best Lawyers and Buffalo News. Analytics properly installed. Nothing here needs rebuilding.
The Drag
An unfinished rebrand in the layer only machines read. Old firm name in hundreds of title tags, dead social accounts declared as official, a robots.txt pointing at a 404, and a legacy domain that drops every deep link. Fifty years of accumulated authority is leaking through a broken join.
The Open Flank
Zero presence in AI answers across every buyer-intent question tested, including the firm's strongest practice. Solo practitioners with a fraction of the resources are being recommended instead, because they answer the question directly and Tiveron does not.
The Finding That Explains The Rest

A title tag is the one line a search engine and an AI assistant read before anything else. On this site, the title tag of a typical article reads: "Egregious Sexual Assault Case Rocks Western New York Community: HoganWillig Law Firm - Amherst, Buffalo, Ellicottville, Lancaster, Lockport New York". We sampled 40 articles and found the old firm name in 39 of them. The sitemap lists 688 articles in total.

Meanwhile the firm's own Organization record, the structured data block that tells machines who this business is, declares its official social profiles as facebook.com/hoganwillig and x.com/hoganwillig. The X account returns a 404. The correct Tiveron accounts are linked in the visible footer, where humans can see them, but not in the markup, where machines look. Every automated system reading this site is being told, consistently and in the firm's own words, that it is looking at HoganWillig.

04 Online Presence Audit
Hosting & InfrastructureA-
WP Engine managed WordPress behind Cloudflare, HTTP/2, a 60ms server response, valid SSL, and a correct 301 from the bare domain to www. Genuinely professional. Only marks off for the complete absence of security headers (no HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options or Referrer-Policy), which is a hygiene rather than a performance issue.
Mobile & Page ExperienceB-
The viewport is set correctly and does not lock pinch-to-zoom, unlike every other site in the portfolio. Lazy-loading is handled in JavaScript. The weak point is images: homepage photographs are served as PNGs at 350KB to 420KB each where WebP would cut them by roughly two thirds, and there is not a single WebP or AVIF file anywhere on the page.
Brand Consistency (Rebrand)F
The headline failure. 39 of 40 sampled articles carry "HoganWillig Law Firm" in the title tag, out of 688 total. The Organization schema and the article:publisher tag both declare HoganWillig social accounts as official, site-wide. At least one live article URL still contains the slug hoganwillig-pllc-attorneys-receive-super-lawyers-recognition. Nearly four years after the rebrand.
Legacy Domain & Link EquityF
On hoganwillig.com the homepage 301-redirects correctly to Tiveron. Every deep URL we tested returns a 404, including /amherst/, /lockport/, /real-estate-law/ and /attorneys/. Google still surfaces some of those old pages in results. For a firm with decades of citations, press coverage and directory links, this discards the accumulated authority of the old domain page by page.
Structured Data / SchemaD+
Present but skeletal. Every page carries WebPage, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and Organization. The Organization block contains only name, URL, logo and those wrong social links: no address, no telephone, no geo coordinates, no service area, no rating. The firm is typed as a generic Organization rather than LegalService or Attorney. No Person markup on 23 attorney bios. No Review markup anywhere.
FAQ Pages (108 of them)F
The clearest missed opportunity on the site. There are 108 individual FAQ pages, each answering a real client question. Every one we sampled shares the identical <h1> of "Frequently Asked Questions" rather than the question itself, ships a meta description consisting of two full stops (..), and carries no FAQPage schema at all. FAQ markup is the primary driver of AI citation. The content exists; the labels do not.
Technical SEO (core pages)C+
Main pages are handled properly: unique descriptive titles, real meta descriptions, canonical tags and Open Graph, all managed by Yoast SEO Premium. But robots.txt declares the sitemap as hoganwillig.com/sitemap_index.xml, which returns a 404, while the site's own working sitemap goes undeclared. Every one of the 688 articles also shares an identical lastmod date, which flattens the freshness signal crawlers use.

How the AI Visibility Result Was Measured, and Its Limits

That is strong evidence of what the engines currently surface, but it is not the same as a screenshot-verified sweep of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and Google AI Overviews in logged-out sessions, which is the standard we hold ourselves to. Read every result here as "not visible from outside" rather than "does not exist". Recommendation 01 is for the firm to run the same prompts and screenshot what comes back, which takes about ten minutes and settles the question with the firm's own evidence rather than ours.

05 Priority Recommendations
01 Start Here. Run the Test Yourself, It Takes Ten Minutes
Before acting on anything below, go and see it. Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and a plain Google search (for the AI Overview at the top), use a private or logged-out window so your own history does not colour the results, and type what a client would actually type. For example: "best real estate closing attorney in Buffalo NY" · "who can help me with a divorce in Western New York" · "recommend an estate planning and elder law firm near Amherst New York" · "how much does a residential real estate closing attorney cost in Buffalo" · and finally "what do you know about Tiveron Law in Buffalo New York". Note which firms get named, and whether Tiveron is one of them. That last prompt is the revealing one: it tests whether the engines know the firm exists at all under its current name. Screenshot what you find. Everything else in this audit explains those screenshots, and the same five prompts re-run monthly are how you measure whether the work is landing.
02 Finish the Rebrand in the Title Tags
This is the highest-leverage item on the audit and it is a settings change, not a rewrite. The article title template still appends "HoganWillig Law Firm - Amherst, Buffalo, Ellicottville, Lancaster, Lockport New York" to every post. In Yoast SEO, edit the title template for the post type once and all 688 articles update together. While in there, do the same for the FAQ post type, where the pattern is also still wrong. Then resubmit the sitemap in Search Console so the corrections are picked up rather than waiting on a natural recrawl. One afternoon of work touching several hundred pages.
03 Redirect the Old Domain Properly, Page by Page
Right now only hoganwillig.com's homepage redirects and every deeper URL 404s, which throws away inbound links built over decades. Because the URL structure largely carried over, most of this can be handled with a single wildcard rule mapping hoganwillig.com/anything/ to tiveronlaw.com/anything/, with a short manual list for the paths that changed. Pull the old domain's top pages from Search Console or a backlink tool first so the highest-value URLs are verified individually. This recovers authority the firm has already paid for.
04 Fix the Organization Record and Point robots.txt at the Right Sitemap
Two small edits with outsized effect. First, the Organization schema: swap the dead HoganWillig social URLs for the live Tiveron accounts, change the type from generic Organization to LegalService, and add what is currently missing entirely, which is address, telephone, geographic coordinates and service area. Second, robots.txt currently directs every crawler to hoganwillig.com/sitemap_index.xml, which returns a 404. Point it at the site's own working sitemap. Both are configuration, not development.
05 Turn 108 Orphaned FAQs Into the Firm's AI Engine
There are already 108 pages answering real client questions, and each is currently wasted three ways: the <h1> says "Frequently Asked Questions" instead of the question, the meta description is literally two full stops, and there is no FAQPage schema. Fix the template so the question becomes the heading, generate descriptions from the first line of each answer, and wrap every question and answer pair in FAQPage markup. Then group them onto the relevant practice pages so a divorce enquiry meets the twelve matrimonial FAQs at once. FAQ markup is the single strongest driver of AI citation, and this firm has more raw FAQ content than any competitor named in this audit.
06 Publish the Answers That Are Winning for Everyone Else
Clark Peshkin, a solo practitioner, beats a 35-attorney firm on the closing question largely by publishing a flat $600 fee. A specific number is a fact an assistant can quote; "contact us for a consultation" is not. The firm's strongest claim, that it handles more residential closings than any other real estate group in the Buffalo area, appears in directory copy but not as a prominent, marked-up statement on the real estate pages. Put the claim, the volume and a real fee range on the page. Do the same for the other practices: what an uncontested divorce typically costs, what a simple will costs, how long an SSD appeal takes. These are exactly the questions in the prompts above.
07 Make 191 Reviews Machine-Readable, and Complete the Map Footprint
The 4.2 rating from 191 reviews is the firm's best competitive asset and no machine can currently see it. Add aggregateRating and Review markup to the testimonials page and the relevant practice pages. Separately, three profile issues need owner-side attention: the Lockport office is not visible in Maps despite having a real street address at 770 Davison Road and should be claimed or created; the Buffalo profile lists the Amherst phone number, which is a direct contradiction of the website and a signal Google penalises; and the Buffalo profile's 3.6 from 17 reviews deserves a deliberate response effort. Worth confirming internally which profiles the firm already manages before any are treated as missing.
08 Clean Up the Directories That AI Actually Cites
When an assistant is asked to recommend a Buffalo lawyer, it leans on Expertise.com, Justia, Super Lawyers, Lawyers.com, Avvo and Yelp far more than on any firm's own website. Several of those listings still resolve under hoganwillig-pllc URLs, which splits the firm's identity in exactly the layer that matters most. Work through them one by one: update the name, confirm the five office addresses and their correct individual phone numbers, add the current attorney roster and the Super Lawyers and Best Lawyers recognitions. This is unglamorous and it is where the recommendations are actually being made.
09 Mark Up the Attorneys and Compress the Images
Two smaller items to close out. The 23 attorney bios are substantial, some above 2,000 words, and carry no Person or Attorney schema, so credentials, bar admissions, practice areas and awards are invisible as structured facts. Adding it makes each attorney an entity an assistant can recognise and cite by name. Separately, homepage photographs are served as PNGs between 350KB and 420KB with no WebP anywhere on the site. Converting them cuts roughly two thirds of the weight on the firm's most-visited page, which is straightforward work on hosting this capable.

The firm rebranded. The website still introduces itself as HoganWillig.

Tiveron has the scale, the reviews and the content library to be the default answer in Western New York. It is losing buyer-intent questions to solo practitioners because its own site tells machines the wrong name, drops its own history at a 404, and leaves 108 ready-made answers unlabelled. Every item above is naming, markup and redirects on work that already exists. Not a rebuild.
