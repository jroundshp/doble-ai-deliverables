Decide which number is right for each office and make the site and the profile match. Separately, the Buffalo profile's 3.6 from 17 reviews deserves a deliberate response effort on its own terms.
08 Clean Up the Directories That AI Actually Cites
When an assistant is asked to recommend a Buffalo lawyer, it leans on Expertise.com, Justia, Super Lawyers, Lawyers.com, Avvo and Yelp far more than on any firm's own website. Re-checked 10 September, and the picture is more mixed than we first reported. The renaming is genuinely under way: the Lawyers.com Buffalo listing is now fully Tiveron, and FindLaw and Lawyers.com Amherst display the current name. But they display it at hoganwillig-pllc URLs, and two listings are still branded HoganWillig outright: the Super Lawyers firm page for Amherst, and the Lawyers.com listing for Ellicottville. There also appear to be duplicate Super Lawyers firm records, one under each name, which is worth resolving before anything else since duplicates split the signal further. Work through them one by one: update the name, confirm the five office addresses and their correct individual phone numbers, add the current attorney roster and the Super Lawyers and Best Lawyers recognitions. This is unglamorous and it is where the recommendations are actually being made.
09 Mark Up the Attorneys and Compress the Images
Two smaller items to close out. The 23 attorney bios are substantial, some above 2,000 words, and carry no Person or Attorney schema, so credentials, bar admissions, practice areas and awards are invisible as structured facts. Adding it makes each attorney an entity an assistant can recognise and cite by name. Separately, homepage photographs are served as PNGs between 350KB and 420KB with no WebP anywhere on the site. Converting them cuts roughly two thirds of the weight on the firm's most-visited page, which is straightforward work on hosting this capable.
10 Bing Verification Is Already in Place — Find the Account and Use It
Unlike every other site in this portfolio, tiveronlaw.com already serves an msvalidate.01 tag, confirmed in a rendered browser on 5 September and again on 10 September 2026. ChatGPT's search runs on Bing's index, not Google's, so this is a real asset and the firm is ahead of its own portfolio here. The 10 September pass also found a google-site-verification tag alongside it, which answers a question we asked in August and never had answered: somebody has verified this domain in Google Search Console, and somebody holds that login. What neither tag tells us is who, or whether a sitemap has ever been submitted to either tool. Track down the account, get the sitemap in, and read the coverage numbers there alongside Search Console. That matters for this firm in particular: the renaming and redirect work above has to land in both indexes, and a 404 on the firm's own history is the kind of thing one index can keep serving long after the other has dropped it.
