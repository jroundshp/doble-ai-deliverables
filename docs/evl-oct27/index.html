02

What the last two Fall Fests tell us

  • Saturday fills itself. Alpine's Fall Fest Saturday grew in 2026 with little Saturday-specific promotion. Saturday posts should raise the check and send people back on Sunday, not try to bring in more people.
  • Sunday and Monday are the extra money. Alpine's Sunday was flat both years, and its Sunday afternoon drink sales dropped sharply in 2026. Columbus Day Monday runs well above a normal Monday, and nobody posted for it.
  • Alpine's Fall Fest is a drinks weekend. Alcohol was Alpine's largest sales category over the 2025 Fall Fest weekend. The best sellers were the pumpkin spice martini, apple cider mimosa, caramel apple on the rocks, espresso martini and spiced apple old fashioned.
  • The Bake Shop runs out before it runs out of customers. In 2025 the Sunday croissants were gone by 9:51 AM, and sales dropped off after noon. In a normal week, a large share of Bake Shop sales comes in before 10 AM (2026 to date).
  • Specifics beat atmosphere. The Bake Shop's 2026 posts named items, gave hours and ran an offer (a free cookie for the first 50 guests once the rain started). Posts that name a drink, give its price and say when to come give people a reason to walk over.
  • Nothing was measurable. No 2026 offer had its own register button, so nobody can say what any single post brought in. That's the easiest thing to fix.
  • How we checked the posting. We also reviewed every Facebook and Instagram post from both shops from Sep 23 through the festival weekend, plus the Stories on Grace's list, against hourly register sales.

03

What each shop posts about

Alpine Bistro · 16 Washington St

The place to sit down, have a drink and catch the game

  • Fall cocktails by name and price. These lead the posts during the festival. Show the drink, the price and the hours it's served.
  • Sunday brunch drinks. Mimosas and boozy coffee, with the time the bar opens.
  • Football. The actual matchup and kickoff time, with food and drinks. Post it the morning of the game, not overnight.
  • The food. The Mediterranean menu and the vendor breakfast, so Alpine reads as lunch too, not only coffee.
  • Coffee and buckets. Good for the morning crowd and the lead-up, but not the main message on the weekend itself.
  • Beer to go. Saturday evening and Sunday afternoon, for the night in and the drive home.
  • The apartment upstairs. In the lead-up and for the weekend after, for people deciding whether to stay.
  • Private events. One post after Fall Fest: book the cocktail room for a holiday party.

Ellicottville Bake Shop · 5 E. Washington St, Suite 5A

The first stop of the day

  • Mornings. Out-of-the-oven photos at opening, breakfast sandwiches, "come early" on Sunday.
  • Seasonal and spiked lattes. Named with ingredients, the way the 2026 spiked latte post did it.
  • Preorders. Pastry boxes and sourdough loaves to take home, ordered ahead so Sunday doesn't sell out by 10.
  • Lunch. Sandwiches for people who want lunch before the craft show.
  • Rain plan. Bring back the free cookie for the first 50 guests on a wet day.
  • Creators. Local food creators posting about and tagging the Bake Shop did well in 2026. Line up two or three for the weekend before.
  • Catering and wholesale. One post after Fall Fest: office and holiday orders.

How we'll know what worked

  • A register button for every offer. The Sunday card, the vendor breakfast, the rain cookie, preorder boxes, beer to go and each festival cocktail all get their own button.
  • No lump-sum entries. Outside stations ring items, not a custom amount at the end of the day.
  • A Monday-after recap. Sales by hour against 2026 for both shops, every offer's count, and which posts and Stories went out when. One page, by Wednesday.
  • Save the Stories. Add the festival Stories to a "Fall Fest" Highlight on each profile, so they're still there to review afterward.

To decide before September 1

  1. Fall Festival dates. Confirm Oct 9–10 with the Chamber.

  3. Bake Shop Sunday production. Bake more for Sunday, push preorders, or both.
  4. Who posts the weekend Stories. One person per shop, on site, each festival day.
  5. The Sunday offer and the ad budget. What the Saturday card gives people, and how much goes into the Fall Fest week ad.
