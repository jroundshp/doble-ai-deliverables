How we post on Fenimore's Facebook and Instagram

More of us can post to @fenimorestable now, which is good. This page is so every post goes up the same way no matter whose phone it came from.

For everyone with posting access to the Fenimore's Table Facebook page and Instagram account.
The short version is right below. Keep this link on your phone. The details and the copy-and-paste captions are further down.

The short version · every post

  1. Check the planner in Meta Business Suite so you don't double up.
  2. Real photo taken at Fenimore's. Hold the phone upright.
  3. Tag the other accounts in it, and add the location "Fenimore's Table."
  4. Caption: the news in the first line, two to four sentences, then the sign-off.
  5. Link: Facebook, paste it. Instagram, "More details in our bio."
  6. Instagram only: the four hashtags at the end.
  7. Post it, then immediately share it to the story on both.
  8. Switch to your personal account and like it. Share it if you want.
  9. Answer the comments and messages that come in that day.

02

The step by step

Do it in this order. The caption comes near the end on purpose: once the photo, tags and location are set, the caption mostly writes itself.

  1. Check the planner

    Open Meta Business Suite and tap Planner. If something is already scheduled within a couple of hours of when you want to post, hold yours for the next gap, or ask Shelley whether yours should replace it. Fifteen seconds.

  2. Pick the photo

    Hold the phone upright, not sideways. Instagram crops a sideways photo into a narrow strip, and the food ends up at the edges. Take a few and use the best one. Natural light beats flash, so a plate by the front windows beats a plate at the pass.

    Up to ten photos in one post is fine (a carousel). Put the strongest one first, because that's the only one most people see.

  3. Tag the accounts in it

    On Instagram, tap Tag people on the photo. On Facebook, type @ in the caption and pick the page from the list. Tag:

    • @cooperstownexperience on anything tied to tournament families, a team dinner, or a Cooperstown Experience event.
    • @cooperstownclubhouse when the post involves the Clubhouse. It's closed for the season now, so this comes back in the spring.
    • A brewery, farm or supplier whose product is in the photo. Search the name in the app and check it's the real account (right town, real posts) before you tag it.
    • Staff or guests only if they said yes.

    When the post matters to the other account too, use Invite collaborator on Instagram instead of a plain tag. If they accept, the same post shows on both profiles and their followers see it. For a Cooperstown Experience team dinner, that's the move.

  4. Add the location

    Every post. Choose Fenimore's Table if it comes up in the list, and Cooperstown, New York if it doesn't. Visitors planning a trip browse the Cooperstown location page, and that's how they find us rather than the place down the street.

  5. Write the caption

    First line is the news: "Sunday Football, noon to 9." Facebook cuts everything after the first line or two behind "See more," so don't warm up. Two to four sentences, then the link line (see section 03), then the sign-off block.

    Prices, hours and specials go in only if they're on the current menu or Shelley has confirmed them. A wrong price in a post is a wrong price at the register.

  6. Instagram only: hashtags

    These four, at the end, every time. Leave them off Facebook.

    #Cooperstown #CooperstownNY #FenimoresTable #OtsegoLake

    During tournament season add #CooperstownBaseball. Skip the long lists of thirty. They look like spam and don't help.

  7. Post it, or schedule it

    Same-day photo: post it now. Anything for a later date: schedule it in Business Suite and it'll show in the planner for everyone. On Instagram, if it's a video, tap Edit cover on the last screen and pick a frame that isn't blurry.

  8. Immediately share it to the story

    Right after it goes up, still signed in as Fenimore's Table. Before anything else.

    Instagram: tap the paper airplane under the post, then Add to story. Before you share, add a Link sticker (the sticker icon at the top) and point it at the page the post is about. A story link is the only tappable link on Instagram besides the bio, so use it.

    Facebook: tap Share under the post, then Share to story.

    Why it matters: plenty of our followers watch stories and never scroll down far enough to hit the post. It takes about ten seconds for each one.

  9. Switch to your personal account and like it

    Find the post from your own Facebook and your own Instagram and like it. Everyone with access does this on every Fenimore's post, not just the person who posted it. If you want, share it to your personal Facebook too. The like is every time; the share is your call.

    Facebook and Instagram decide how many people see a post partly from how fast people react to it in the first hour. Our personal accounts know a lot more people in Otsego County than the restaurant page does, so a few quick likes from staff push the post in front of their friends.

  10. Facebook groups, for specials and events

    Bob already shares Fenimore's specials to the Celebrate Cooperstown and Milford Facebook groups, where posts wait for the group admin to approve them. If you want to share something there, check with Bob first so the group doesn't get it twice.

  11. Answer comments and messages that day

    "Are you open for breakfast?" and "can I book a table for twelve?" come in as comments and messages. Whoever posted watches them that day. A question answered in an hour usually turns into a visit, and one answered on Thursday usually doesn't. If you don't know the answer, say you'll check, and ask Shelley.

03

Links work differently on each one

This is the rule that trips people up most.

Facebook

Paste the link right in the post

A web address in a Facebook post can be tapped. Paste the full address on its own line near the end, before the sign-off.

The ones we use:

  • https://www.fenimorestable.com/breakfast/
  • https://www.fenimorestable.com/menu/
  • https://www.fenimorestable.com/drinks/
  • https://www.fenimorestable.com/private-events/
  • Reservations and online ordering: use the Reserve a Table and Order Online buttons on fenimorestable.com. Copy the address from there.

Instagram

Links in a caption don't work

If you paste a web address into an Instagram caption, it shows up as plain text nobody can tap. So don't paste it. End the caption with:

More details in our bio.

That sends people to the profile, where the bio ends with More details 👇 and the link sits right underneath it. The finger points at the one spot on Instagram that can be tapped.

Fenimore's Table
Cooperstown, NY
Elevated dining & craft cocktails
More details 👇
🔗 order.toasttab.com/online/fenimorestable and 1 more

The story link sticker (step 8) is the other tappable spot.

One-time setup, for Grace. As of 14 September the @fenimorestable bio reads Cooperstown, NY / Elevated dining & craft cocktails with no "More details 👇" line yet. Add it as the last line of the bio (Edit profile, then Bio) so the link lands right under the finger. The bio links for ordering and reservations stay as they are. Nobody else needs to touch the profile.

06

A few don'ts

  • Don't post as Fenimore's from your personal profile, or post Fenimore's news on your personal profile first. Page first, then share.
  • Don't paste links into Instagram captions.
  • Don't reply to a bad review or an angry comment in the moment. Screenshot it and send it to Shelley. A calm answer the next morning reads better than a fast one.
  • Don't ask for or pass around passwords. If you can't get in, message Grace.
