You're putting your time into your database for the next few months. Here are two ways to handle the website and social while you do. Either one works.

01

You take the keys

We move everything into accounts in your name. You own and control it, and you decide what to do with it, if anything, until spring.

What you get
  • The hosting account and the site's code, transferred to you
  • The domain, johntylerrealestate.com, set up to point at your hosting
  • Owner access to Google Analytics, Search Console and the contact form
  • A one-page guide listing every account, what it does and where it lives
What comes with it
  • Hosting is billed to you. A paid Vercel plan runs about $20 a month
  • The site has no editing login. Changing text, listings or posts takes someone who is comfortable working in code
  • Our daily MLS check stops, so new listings, price changes and sales won't show up on the site unless someone updates them
  • The domain is up for renewal on March 30, 2027

We keep paying for hosting and keep the site running through October 31 while the handoff happens. After that, you can still hire us for anything at $150 an hour.
