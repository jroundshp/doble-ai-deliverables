4Schedule it in Meta, then let it run

Once the team signs off on the calendar and graphics, every post goes into Meta Business Suite with its photo, caption and link, scheduled for its date and time on Facebook and Instagram together. A post scheduled in November goes out on Christmas Eve whether or not anyone is near a phone.

  • A month ahead. December is fully scheduled by November 20; January by December 18.
  • Game posts go out the morning of the game; the 8:15 and 8:20 night games get a second post at 3pm.
  • One change rule. If hours or a special change, the Marketing Team edits the scheduled post in Meta. Nobody posts a correction on top of it.

5Boots on the ground

The scheduled posts keep a steady rhythm. The people at Fenimore's make it feel alive: a full room on a Bills Sunday, a plate the kitchen is proud of, a party setup before guests arrive.

  • When it makes sense, post it. The moments a schedule can't plan: a famous ballplayer stops in for dinner, a party or team group in full swing, a packed room for a Bills game, a great happy hour shot. Stories are the easy default; a feed post is fine when the photo is great.
  • Ask first. Before posting a guest, a group or a visiting player, get their OK. Most say yes, and a tag from them reaches their followers too.
  • Swap, don't stack. If a same-day photo beats the scheduled one, swap it in Meta rather than running both.
  • Real photos only, taken on site. Each person posts with their own login; nobody shares a password.
  • Who: name one person for each stretch of the week (question for the team).
