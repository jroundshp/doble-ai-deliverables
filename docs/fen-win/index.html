Winter plan · Fenimore's Table and Cooperstown Clubhouse

Cooperstown this winter: two objectives

Prepared 17 September 2026 by Doble AI, from both websites as they read that morning. Status updated 18 September 2026 after a full recheck of the live Clubhouse site: 12 of the 21 fix list items are done and two more are part done.

The Clubhouse has closed for the season, and Fenimore's is open all year. So the digital work in Cooperstown has two jobs, and most of it can be done in the next four weeks: get the Clubhouse ready for a strong spring opening, and, more important right now, send every search, click and call that still reaches the Clubhouse over to Fenimore's and its events.

Why this, and why right now

The Clubhouse still gets found while it's closed

People keep searching for it, calling it and landing on its website over the winter. Right now all of that ends at a closed door with no next step. Fenimore's, also on Route 28, has the same owners, the same culinary director and the same inbox, so each of those people could become a Fenimore's guest instead.

Fenimore's events push has just started, and holiday parties book in the fall

The Private Events page and its inquiry form went live on 15 September. On the 14 September call the team said pre-booking for holiday parties is already running late. Group and team parties are what the Clubhouse is known for, so its audience is a good fit for Fenimore's events.

The site says two different things

The Clubhouse homepage says "Closed for the season", but the footer on every page still lists Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and the Activities page says "Arcade Now Open!" A guest who believes the hours, drives out and finds it closed is the kind of guest who leaves a bad review, and that review stays on the listing into spring.

A deadline in December, and no reason to wait for it

The old rookiescrafthouseny.com domain expires on 19 December 2026. The Clubhouse site still loads 19 of its images from it, and its "Order Online" and "Book Team Party" buttons point there. If it lapses, those images and links break mid-winter, when nobody is watching the site.

Is this a chance to promote events? Yes.

The Clubhouse built its name on group and team parties, from 30 to 250 people. The people who look it up are often planning exactly the kind of gathering Fenimore's now wants to host, so the winter message should lead with events rather than only "we're closed":

What to promote

  • Holiday parties, which book now
  • Team dinners and end-of-season banquets, which the Clubhouse Teams page already invites
  • Rehearsal dinners, showers, birthdays and work gatherings
  • One link for all of them: fenimorestable.com/private-events

Limits to be honest about

  • Fenimore's seats 95 indoors. The Clubhouse's larger groups used the patio and the tent, so they won't fit in winter.
  • Package prices aren't final yet, so the message asks people to send an inquiry rather than quoting prices.
  • Whether a team can walk in after a game is still an open question, so the copy says "plan ahead" for now.

Most of this takes four weeks. The closed messages and the links to Fenimore's go up this week, the events push starts next week, and the Clubhouse's spring list is finished by mid-October. After that the only work left is the reopening itself, whenever that date is set.
