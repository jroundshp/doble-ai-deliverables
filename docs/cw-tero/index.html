1. Call the TERO office first (Thu 8 or Fri 9 Oct)

Out of respect for Indian preference, TERO hears from us before anyone else does. Ask:

  • Status of CWS's license (type, number, expiry)? CWS is not on any TERO list posted today, and TERO 101 is for licensed firms only. The vendor/supplier license (10-J) is $2,500; card payments carry a 3% fee.
  • How does CWS get on the Approved Oilfield Vendor list?
  • Does a booth include registration? Can a company register for the Expo before its license is issued?
  • Does VPC need a supplier license? TERO keeps a "Suppliers (Non Native)" list.
  • Can TERO introduce us to certified Indian contractors?

Licensing is in person, Mon to Thu, owners present.

2. How tribal work is awarded

Competitive tribal bids are limited to certified Indian contractors. A certified Tier 1 firm within 2% of the low bid wins, at any contract size. (The regulations' 10% figure for projects under $1M is a test of price against the buyer's own estimate, not a bid margin.) Routes in for a supplier:

  1. Sell to GCs as a supplier.
  2. Sign a Mentorship Agreement with a certified firm (gives that firm Preference Level 2). A firm may hold at most four, and an agreement cannot cover a service the certified firm performs itself. TERO announced audits of these agreements on 17 Feb 2026.

Risks

  • Preference rules can shut a non-certified firm out of tribal bids.
  • Fees: the 2022 TERO Ordinance charges 2.5% on construction contracts of $1,000 or more. The Ordinance (§601(b)) also charges 2.5% of quarterly reservation payroll to non-construction firms with five or more employees on the Reservation or $1,000 or more in gross sales there, so a supplier is covered. The 2026 regulations list 0.5% of quarterly payroll for a business based on the reservation, while the Ordinance says 2.5%; confirm which applies.
  • Vehicles: TERO's Marking of Vehicles regulation (12 May 2026) requires both sides of company vehicles to show "Owner" and the legal name on the TERO or MHA DOT license; penalty up to $500, and up to $500 a day if it continues. TERO says the rule is being revised; ask.
  • Watford City and Stanley are off-reservation, so no TERO there.
