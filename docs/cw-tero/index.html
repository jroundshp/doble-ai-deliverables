05 · Preparation

How to get the most out of it

1. Call the TERO office first (Thu 8 or Fri 9 Oct)

Out of respect for Indian preference, TERO hears from us before anyone else does. Ask:

  • Status of CWS's license (type, number, expiry)? CWS is not on any TERO list posted today, and TERO 101 is for licensed firms only. The vendor/supplier license (10-J) is $2,500; card payments carry a 3% fee.
  • How does CWS get on the Approved Oilfield Vendor list?
  • Does a booth include registration?
  • Does VPC need a supplier license? TERO keeps a "Suppliers (Non Native)" list.
  • Can TERO introduce us to certified Indian contractors?
  • New: no certified firm supplies concrete at any preference level. Will TERO confirm that buyers may open concrete bids to non-certified suppliers under Regs §3.1B and §3.2(g)?
  • New: if an owner is an enrolled MHA member, what does certification require, and how long does it take?

Licensing is in person, Mon to Thu, owners present.

2. Partnering path for CWS

Competitive tribal bids are limited to certified Indian contractors. A certified Tier 1 firm within 2% of the low bid wins, at any contract size. (The regulations' 10% figure for projects under $1M is a test of price against the buyer's own estimate, not a bid margin.) If CWS is not Indian-owned:

  1. Sell where no certified firm competes. There is no concrete or ready-mix category at any preference level. When no certified firm offers a service, the regulations let the buyer open the bid to others (§3.1B with TERO director approval; §3.2(g)).
  2. Sell to GCs as a supplier.
  3. Sign a Mentorship Agreement with a certified firm (gives that firm Preference Level 2). A firm may hold at most four, and an agreement cannot cover a service the certified firm performs itself. TERO began auditing these in Feb 2026.
  4. Form a joint venture with an MHA member, with counsel.

Risks

  • Preference rules can shut a non-certified firm out of tribal bids.
  • Fees: the 2022 TERO Ordinance charges 2.5% on construction contracts of $1,000 or more. Whether materials-only sales count is not stated; ask. The 2026 regulations list 0.5% of quarterly payroll for a business based on the reservation, while the Ordinance says 2.5%; confirm which applies.
  • Contractor vehicles: the brief says they must be registered with MHA DOT. We could not find that rule; TERO says its vehicle-marking regulation is being revised. Ask.
  • Watford City and Stanley are off-reservation, so no TERO there.
