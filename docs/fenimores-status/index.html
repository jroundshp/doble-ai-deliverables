03

A correction to the 9 September readout

The sitemap does not resolve, and our last note said it did. That note reported robots.txt now pointing at sitemap_index.xml and listing the five real pages. robots.txt does point there. The file itself returns 404, and so does every other address it could live at: wp-sitemap.xml, sitemap.xml, page-sitemap.xml and rankmath-sitemap.xml. Re-checked uncached on 10 September.

The practical effect is that the one file telling Google where to find the site's pages is currently pointing at a dead address, and there is no sitemap behind it. On a five-page site Google will find the pages anyway, so this is not urgent. It is worth closing because it is a two-minute fix and because a dead sitemap declaration is the kind of thing that stays broken for a year.

Rank Math is installed and its sitemap module is not serving. Its settings are not reachable through this site's API, so this is a toggle in the WordPress dashboard rather than a code change.

Three public surfaces are currently selling a Monday dinner. If Monday is a 4PM close, then a local who checks the website or Google and drives out on a Monday evening finds the kitchen shut. That is the single most expensive thing on this page, because the whole point of the autumn push is to bring in people who live here, and a local who makes that drive once does not make it twice.

Nothing else in this section can be corrected until somebody says which is true, because the answer has to be written into the page text, the schema and the Google profile in one pass. One sentence unblocks all three.

Separate from the Monday question, and fixable the moment the above is answered:

  • The footer on the same page publishes Tues to Wed, 6:30AM to 4PM.
  • The schema on the same page publishes Tu,We 06:30-16:00, which is 4PM.

Two of the three agree, so the body block is the outlier. This is the same defect that was found and fixed on the contact page yesterday, in a second place. Verified uncached on 10 September.

Changes worth making

  1. Monday, once the dinner question is answered

    If dinner is Thursday to Sunday, Monday needs to move to a 4PM close here at the same time it moves on the site. Doing one without the other puts the two surfaces back out of step.

  2. Add “Serves breakfast” to the profile's attributes

    This is the highest-value item on the list. Breakfast is the new daypart and “serves breakfast” is a filter people actually use. The public panel today shows Takeout and Wheelchair accessible. Dine-in, serves breakfast, serves lunch, serves dinner, accepts reservations, outdoor seating, good for kids, alcohol and parking are not visible on it. Some of those may already be set and simply not surfaced to a signed-out visitor, so this is a list to confirm in the dashboard rather than a list of known gaps.

  3. Add a secondary category

    The profile is categorised as Restaurant and nothing else. Restaurant should stay as the primary. Adding Breakfast restaurant as a secondary is how a profile surfaces in a breakfast search, and it costs nothing.

  4. Publish a Post about breakfast

    No Updates or Posts are visible on the profile. A Google Post is the cheapest surface Corey owns on this property, it shows directly in the profile panel, and a new 6:30AM breakfast service with a new card is exactly what it is for. This is also the surface that reaches somebody typing “breakfast near me” while sitting in a car on Route 28.

  5. Seed the Questions and Answers

    The profile carries no questions. An owner can post and answer their own, and those answers show in the panel and get read by assistants. The ones every inquiry opens with: do you serve breakfast and from what time, is there parking, do you take reservations, are you open to the public, what time does lunch start, do you have a private room and how many does it seat.

  6. Set the price level

    The profile carries no price band. The site's own schema says $$. It is a filter in Google Maps and it takes one click.

  7. Confirm every review has a reply

    58 reviews, of which 36 are five stars and 7 are one star. Whether the one-star reviews carry owner replies was not visible on a signed-out check, so this is a confirmation rather than a finding. Replying to all of them is free and it is read by both people and assistants.

None of the above needs a developer. All of it is inside the Google Business Profile dashboard.

6:30 is a tie, not a lead. Do not build a campaign on being first to open, because two other places open at the same minute and one of them is a coffee shop that has been doing it for years. Published hours were read from each listing; they were not confirmed by standing outside the door.

What Fenimore's has that none of those four have is Route 28, a parking lot and a hundred seats. Every one of the others is in the village, where parking is the thing people complain about. If there is a line to run on breakfast, that is the line, and it is a stronger one than the clock.

One conflict to settle before the competitive set is used again: the 9 September call recorded Cooperstown Diner as open three days a week, and its published hours read Wednesday to Sunday. Also from that call, and already applied: Nicoletta's has been closed for six to eight months.
