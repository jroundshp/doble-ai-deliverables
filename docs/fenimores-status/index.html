01

The short version

Before 9 September this website said nothing a machine could read about the restaurant it describes. It published no structured data, no page descriptions, no social cards and no working menu data, and Google's own profile was the only correct source of anything.

That has been rebuilt. The site now states its own hours, address, price band and all 56 dishes with their prices in a form a search engine and an AI assistant can quote. What is left is mostly content nobody has sent yet, plus about an hour of clicks on the Google profile.

05

The Google Business Profile

Checked signed-out on 10 September. The fundamentals are in good shape and better than July: the profile is claimed, rated 4.1★ across 58 reviews, up from 42 in July. Category is Restaurant. The website field points correctly at https://www.fenimorestable.com/, the phone reads (607) 547-2767, the address reads 4874 NY-28, and the hours match the website exactly. A menu link, an online ordering link and a reservations link are all present.

Six changes worth making. None needs a developer, and all of them live inside the Google Business Profile dashboard.

  1. Add “Serves breakfast” to the attributes

    The highest-value item here. Breakfast is the new daypart and this is a filter people actually use. The public panel today shows Takeout and Wheelchair accessible. Dine-in, serves breakfast, serves lunch, serves dinner, accepts reservations, outdoor seating, good for kids, alcohol and parking are not visible on it. Some may already be set and simply not shown to a signed-out visitor, so this is a list to confirm in the dashboard rather than a list of known gaps.

  2. Add a secondary category

    The profile is categorised as Restaurant and nothing else. Restaurant should stay primary. Adding Breakfast restaurant as a secondary is how a profile surfaces in a breakfast search, and it costs nothing.

  3. Publish a Post about breakfast

    No Updates or Posts are visible on the profile. A Google Post appears directly in the profile panel, and a new 6:30AM service with a new card is exactly what it is for. This is the surface that reaches somebody typing “breakfast near me” from a car on Route 28, and it is free.

  4. Seed the Questions and Answers

    The profile carries no questions. An owner can post and answer their own, and those answers show in the panel and get read by AI assistants. The ones every inquiry opens with: do you serve breakfast and from what time, is there parking, do you take reservations, are you open to the public, what time does lunch start, and do you have a private room and how many does it seat.

  5. Set the price level

    The profile carries no price band. The site's own schema says $$. It is a filter in Google Maps and it takes one click.

  6. Confirm every review has a reply

    58 reviews, of which 36 are five stars and 7 are one star. Whether the one-star reviews carry owner replies was not visible on a signed-out check, so this is a confirmation rather than a finding. Replying is free and it is read by both people and assistants.
