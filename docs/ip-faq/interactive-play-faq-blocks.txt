### TriWave Model 100
https://interactivewaterparks.com/products/aquatic-play-structures/triwave-100/

Q: How much space does the TriWave Model 100 need?
A: The TriWave Model 100 requires a minimum footprint of 4,300 sq ft / 399.4 sq m. The unit itself measures 60’ x 45’ x 16’ 7” (L/W/H), with a top deck height of 108 in / 2.74 m.

Q: How many guests can the TriWave Model 100 serve?
A: Up to 172 water players. The structure carries 20 interactive water features and 2 slides – closed flume tube slides (8 ft and 9 ft high).

Q: What kind of pool does the TriWave Model 100 install over?
A: Shallow depth pool 6-8 inches deep. All models can be customized with your selection of water play toys and designed to a footprint and scale that fits your facility.
