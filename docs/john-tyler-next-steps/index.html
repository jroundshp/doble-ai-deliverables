The same five questions, every property

  1. Is there an HOA? If so, what does it cover, what does it run, and is anything assessed. If not, just say no and skip it.
  2. Can it be rented short term, and does the building allow it? The first thing second-home buyers ask and the one they cannot look up.
  3. Where is the parking, is it deeded, how many spaces? Often the deciding factor in Vail and rarely in the listing.
  4. What floor, which exposure, what do you see?
  5. What conveys with the sale?
