The New Town video

Step 8 · forward this section as is

This is the only part of the project that has to happen on site. It is a phone task rather than a computer task, and the whole thing takes about ten minutes including the walk.

Who films it

Amanda or Richard, whoever is at the yard when the link arrives. Forward this section to them as written.

What Google needs to see

Four things, in one continuous take. Do not stop the recording and do not edit it afterward. Google is watching for cuts, because cuts are how people fake this.

  1. The sign, and the road it sits on. Start here. Hold on the sign long enough to read it, then pan to the road so the surroundings are visible. If there is a street sign or a mile marker nearby, catch it.
  2. The yard, walking. Keep moving and keep filming. Stockpiles, the crusher, the batch plant, loaders, whatever is standing. This is what proves it is a materials operation rather than an empty lot.
  3. Something that shows you run it. Climb into the cab and start a machine, or open the office and show paperwork with Concrete Warrior on it, or unlock a gate with your own keys. Google calls this proof of management and it is the part people usually forget.
  4. End wide. One slow pan across the whole operation before you stop recording.

Practical notes for whoever is filming

  • Shoot landscape, phone turned sideways.
  • Two to five minutes is plenty. Under thirty seconds looks thin.
  • Retake it as many times as you want. Nothing is submitted until you press submit, so a bad take costs nothing.
  • Do not narrate for Google's benefit. Nobody is scoring the commentary.

The handover to Corey

What moves, what already lives there, and when

Once the website is built and dialed in, John transfers the domain to Corey's organization and they hold the whole thing. That is the plan and it has been since the domain was bought on 14 August.

Set up the way this document describes, that handover is close to a non-event. Most of what would normally have to change hands never sits with us in the first place.

Timing on the domain

ICANN locks a newly registered domain against a registrar transfer for 60 days, so a move to Corey's own registrar cannot happen before roughly 13 October 2026. Corey already knows and has said it is not a problem.

If a faster route is ever wanted, a team-to-team transfer inside Vercel has no lock at all. That gets Corey's organization holding it immediately while leaving the site where it is.

The thing to decide before, not after

DNS control moves with the domain. Right now John holds it, which is the whole reason he appears in this document at all. After the transfer, anything that needs a DNS record, a new mail alias on a different provider, a subdomain, a verification string for some other service, goes through whoever administers Corey's account rather than through him.

That is fine and it is the point of handing it over. It is worth saying out loud once, because the failure mode is nobody realizing it until something needs changing on a Friday.

Two things worth keeping after the transfer

Doble AI should stay a Manager on the Google Business Profile if there is ongoing findability work, since a Manager cannot delete anything or change ownership. And the Workspace should keep admin@ as a super admin account distinct from anyone's daily login, for the same reason it was created that way: people change jobs and role accounts do not.
