04

Request verification

Breanna

Submit and Google offers a method. You do not get to pick. Three are likely here.

  • Video recording. Most likely for a rural aggregate yard. Google produces a link that opens on any phone. Text it to whoever is at the yard. Step 5.
  • Live video call with an agent. Real time, no retakes. If both are offered, take the recording.
  • Postcard. Best case. A card arrives at the yard with a five digit code, somebody reads it over the phone to Buffalo, done.

Plan for the video, take the postcard gladly if it shows up. Agree with New Town which day suits before you file, because the link is generated on submission and the person at the yard is on the clock from that moment.

05

Record the video at the yard

New Town~10 min

The only part of this that has to happen on site. Forward this step as written to whoever holds the phone, Amanda or Richard.

Why it carries more weight here than usual

Google has no business record at this address. No street imagery of the yard, no auto-generated listing to correct, nothing to lean on. The video is doing the entire job of proving a real operation stands there.

Four things, in one continuous take. Do not stop the recording and do not edit it. Google watches for cuts, because cuts are how people fake this.

  1. The sign, and the road it sits on. Hold on it long enough to read, then pan to the road. Catch a street sign or mile marker if there is one.
  2. The yard, walking. Keep moving and keep filming. Stockpiles, the crusher, the batch plant, loaders. This is what proves it is a materials operation rather than an empty lot.
  3. Something that shows you run it. Climb into a cab and start a machine, open the office and show paperwork with the name on it, or unlock a gate with your own keys. Google calls this proof of management and it is the part people forget.
  4. End wide. One slow pan across the whole operation.
  • Shoot landscape, phone sideways.
  • Two to five minutes. Under thirty seconds looks thin.
  • Retake as many times as you want. Nothing is submitted until you press submit.
  • No need to narrate. Nobody scores the commentary. A windy day is fine, audio does not matter.

If there is no sign, stop and tell Buffalo before filming. A missing sign does not make verification impossible, but it makes it considerably harder and it may be worth ordering one first. That question has been open since 20 August.

Breanna, with John~30 min

A verified but empty profile ranks poorly. This is where the listing actually starts working.

  • Seed the Q&A with what buyers actually ask. Do you deliver. What is the minimum load. Do you carry Class 5. Can you spec to NDDOT.
  • Attributes and the opening date

04

Nine things not to do

Each of these has cost somebody a listing

  • Do not put materials or the town in the business name. "Supply" is a brand decision and is fine if the sign says it. "Ready Mix" and "New Town" are keywords and are not.
  • Do not use (701) 580-3207. That is Patriot's number on a Texas website, and pointing one number at two businesses in two states is how Google decides they are the same listing.
  • Do not create the profile from a personal Google account. Use office@concretewarriorsupply.com.
  • Do not use rhinoreadymix1@gmail.com for anything customer-facing. It carries the name of an entity that is not in good standing.
  • Do not upload photos from Patriot's website.
  • Do not pick "concrete contractor" as a category.
  • Do not touch the Strata Corporation or West Dakota Ready Mix listings. Somebody else's unclaimed profile is not ours to edit.
  • Do not enter a website URL while the site is still returning a 404. Blank beats broken.
  • Do not stop the recording partway through the verification video.

Two things we are launching without

Both matter. Neither is holding anything up.

Saying this plainly is better than leaving them on a list looking like blockers.

Important, added later

Prequalification

ISNetworld, Veriforce or Avetta. Roughly 95% of operators in the basin gate supplier accounts behind one of them, so this carries real commercial weight and is not being dismissed. Registration costs money and picking a platform is an owners' decision, so it comes in as soon as possible or whenever it makes business sense.

It also came off the website on 3 September. The homepage named all three and said none were set up, and the answers section asked the question and left it blank. An estimator reads an unanswered prequalification question as a no, and there is no reason to volunteer that before anybody asks. It goes back on the day there is a registration to name, which is a five-minute change.

No longer a gate

The 40-acre lease

This used to be what kept the website hidden from search. As of 3 September it is not. Attaching the domain and lifting the search block is a decision rather than a date, and the lease gets added to the picture when it is done.

Practically, that means the listing's website field stays blank at creation and gets filled in shortly afterwards, rather than waiting on paperwork nobody controls.

The handover to Corey

Mostly a non-event, by design

Set up the way this document describes, most of what would normally change hands never sits with us in the first place. The Business Profile is already inside Corey's Workspace and Breanna already owns it. The Workspace has been on his billing from day one. The domain is the only real transfer.

Website hostingVercel project concretewarriorsupplyMoves with or after the domain

The thing to decide before, not after

DNS control moves with the domain, and that is the whole reason John appears in this document at all. Afterwards, anything needing a DNS record, a mail alias on a different provider, a subdomain, a verification string for some other service, goes through whoever administers Corey's account. That is the point of handing it over. It is worth saying once, because the failure mode is nobody realising until something needs changing on a Friday.

Two things worth keeping after the transfer: Doble AI stays a Manager on the profile while there is ongoing findability work, since a Manager cannot delete anything or change ownership, and the Workspace keeps admin@ as a super admin distinct from anyone's daily login. People change jobs. Role accounts do not.
