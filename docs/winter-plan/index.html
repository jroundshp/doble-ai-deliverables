Five properties, two towns, one winter

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. A draft to fine-tune together.

The plan covers five properties, and in each town they work as a team. In Cooperstown, the Clubhouse is closed for the winter, so it sends everyone who finds it to Fenimore's and starts booking summer 2027 teams. In Ellicottville, the Bake Shop, Alpine Bistro and the new Huntley House breakfast hand guests to each other through the day, depending on which door is open and what each one sells.

Each property plan has the same five parts: the website, the Google Business Profile, the social platforms, the in-store signs and in-store staff training. All five carry the same message in the same week, set month by month from November to March.

05How the five parts work

The website

Doble AI leads the website updates, edits and changes for all five properties, working with each team. Each plan lists the changes with a date and the person on the team who signs off, timed to land the week the month's message starts.

The Google Business Profile

For many guests the Google profile is the first thing they see, before the website, and often the only thing: hours, directions, photos, a call button. Each plan keeps every property's profile in step with its website: the same hours, holiday hours set ahead of time, the right menu, order and booking links. It also posts a weekly update pre-scheduled from the hub, adds new real photos every month and answers every review. Doble AI leads this with each manager.

The social calendar

Each property gets a channel plan, a target mix of food, drinks, events and cross-promotion, a weekly rhythm with posting times, and dated posts for November to March. All the key posts for every property are built and pre-scheduled from one hub, a month at a time, before the month starts. Designated people at each property, the ones with boots on the ground, add their own posts in between: the room filling up, a special coming out of the kitchen, a party in full swing. Each town has its own events: Mardi Gras Weekend and Winter Blues are Ellicottville's, and the Winter Carnival and Candlelight Evening are Cooperstown's. National holidays go on both calendars. Football is a Fenimore's draw because of its screens; Alpine gets an occasional "Go Bills" and nothing more.

In-store signs

Each plan lists its signs: where each one goes, what it says, when it goes up and when it comes down. QR codes go straight to the page that books or buys. Doble AI can supply print-ready artwork; each property prints with its usual printer.

Staff training

The purpose: every staff member at every location knows their own property and its sister properties. They know what each one offers and when each is open. That way they promote their own location all day and send guests to a sister property whenever it's the better fit: an open door, a product, a room for a group. In Cooperstown, Fenimore's staff also speak for the Clubhouse. In Ellicottville, the Bake Shop, Alpine and Huntley House teams all know each other's hours and menus.

The same schedule everywhere: a 20-minute kickoff huddle the week of October 26, then a 10-minute refresh at the start of each month (December 1, January 4, February 1, March 1). Each property has a one-page card to print and keep at the register, with the hours of the other doors and the exact words to use at closing time.
