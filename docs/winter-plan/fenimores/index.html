Fenimore's Table: fill the winter

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: Fenimore's is the one door open in Cooperstown all winter. The job from November to March is to fill it: book holiday parties and private events, pack the three dinner specials nights, make Fenimore's the place to watch football in Cooperstown, and take every guest the Clubhouse sends over while it's closed.

This page builds on what's already running: the posting playbook, the September to March social calendar and Bob's weekly rhythm. It adds the website, the signs and staff training so all four carry the same message in the same week.

01Where it stands today

Read from the live website and the work done so far, 1 October 2026.

Hours and specials are clear on the website

Tuesday and Wednesday 6:30 AM to 4 PM, Thursday to Monday 6:30 AM to 9 PM. The menu page lists the three weekly specials: Thursday Chicken Riggies $14.95, Friday Fish Fry $16.95, Saturday Prime Rib $27.95.

Private events are set up to book

The Private Events page has four packages, room for up to 95 guests indoors and an inquiry form that Google Analytics counts. Holiday parties are mentioned on the site, but there's no holiday section with dates to book by.

Three things guests can't find on the website

Gift cards, an email sign-up and Sunday football aren't visible on any page. All three matter between Thanksgiving and the Super Bowl.

The Clubhouse already points here

Its website sends visitors to Fenimore's for food, team dinners and online orders. Its Facebook (about 1,400 followers) and Instagram can do the same all winter.

06Staff training

The purpose: everyone at Fenimore's knows two places, not one. They know what Fenimore's offers and when it's open, so they can sell it at every table. They also know the Clubhouse, so they can answer its callers and book its summer 2027 teams. Short huddles before a shift, run by the manager on duty. Everyone working the floor or the phone gets the card.

  • Week of Oct 26: 20-minute kickoff before service. Walk through the card, the holiday party offer and how to take an event inquiry.
  • Dec 1, Jan 4, Feb 1, Mar 1: 10-minute refresh at the start of each month: the new message, the new signs, the dates coming up.
  • Feb 10: 5 minutes on Lent: the Fish Fry, what's in it, what guests ask about.
  • Every new hire: the card on day one.

07How we'll know it's working

  • Private event inquiries per month (the form is already counted in Google Analytics) and parties booked, against last winter.

  • Gift cards sold, Black Friday to Christmas Eve (Toast).
  • Visits to the site from Google, and calls and direction requests from the Google profile.
  • Email sign-ups, once the form is live.
