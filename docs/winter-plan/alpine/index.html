Alpine Bistro: the door that's open longest

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: of the three Ellicottville doors, Alpine is open the most hours and the most days. It serves breakfast and lunch every day and dinner on Friday and Saturday evenings. It also has the drinks and the room for groups and events. The plan makes it the place the other two send people, and grows the three things only it can sell: Friday and Saturday evenings, coffee drinks with alcohol, and groups and private events.

Alpine also sends people the other way. Early risers go to the Bake Shop, which opens at 7:30, and anyone planning a group or a weekend hears about rooms at Huntley House.

06Staff training

Huddles before a shift, run by Leslie or Matt. Everyone at the counter gets the card. The most important part is the hand-off at closing time: telling a guest what's open next.

  • Week of Oct 26: 20-minute kickoff, with the Bake Shop team if possible, so both counters say the same thing about each other.
  • Dec 1, Jan 4, Feb 1, Mar 1: 10-minute monthly refresh: the message, the signs, the dates.
  • Before the first evening push: the coffee cocktails: what's in them, how to offer one, who can be served.
  • Every new hire: the card on day one.
