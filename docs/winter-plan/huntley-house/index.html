Huntley House: the third door

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: launch breakfast at Huntley House as the Bake Shop's third location and make it part of every guest's stay. It's a block from the Bake Shop.

Second objective: send every hotel guest to Alpine Bistro, which is open when they're not at breakfast: for lunch, coffee, après and dinner on Friday and Saturday, and for the group dinner when they travel together. Also send them to the Bake Shop on mornings they'd rather walk.

Corey has said the hotel gives near-complete freedom on the menu and promotion, including an in-room brochure with a QR code. That makes the room itself the main channel.

01Where it stands today

Read from the live website and the work done so far, 1 October 2026.

A start date and hours to confirm

Breakfast was expected to start this fall. The start date, hours and the name on the breakfast ("by the Ellicottville Bake Shop") are still to confirm.

The hotel's own name and channels

We use "Huntley House" throughout. In our August check the hotel's listings still showed Wingate by Wyndham, with the new brand due in spring 2027. Its website, booking listings and social accounts belong to the hotel, so anything there is done with its OK.

The audience

Around 94 rooms, with up to about 300 guests on a busy-season weekend. Most arrive in the afternoon, when Alpine is the only one of the three doors open.
