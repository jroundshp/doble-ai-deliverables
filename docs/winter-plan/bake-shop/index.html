Ellicottville Bake Shop: make every open hour count

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: the Bake Shop is open seven hours a day, 7:30 AM to 2:30 PM, and its mornings carry the business. The plan sells those hours harder: the baked goods and coffee, weekend mornings before the lifts, orders placed ahead and catering. Holiday orders matter most in November and December.

Second objective: send people to Alpine Bistro, which is open longer, serves all three meals, pours the drinks and has room for groups. Every afternoon at 2:30 the Bake Shop hands its customers to Alpine, on social and at the counter.

01Where it stands today

Read from the live website and the work done so far, 1 October 2026.

Hours

Open daily, 7:30 AM to 2:30 PM. The same on the site and in Google's data.

Ordering ahead is already possible

Online ordering through Toast, and catering and wholesale with 48 to 72 hours' notice (a week for big orders), with breakfast and catering menus on the site.

Mornings carry the business

More than 40% of sales come before 10 AM, and a weekend morning brings about twice the orders of a weekday morning.

What the site doesn't show yet

Gift cards, an email sign-up and the hotel breakfast aren't visible on the site. Alpine appears only as a footer line.

08Staff training

The purpose: everyone at the Bake Shop knows all three Ellicottville doors: what the Bake Shop, Alpine and the Huntley House breakfast each offer, and when each is open. They sell the Bake Shop every hour it's open, and they send customers to Alpine for everything the Bake Shop doesn't do: lunch after 2:30, drinks, evenings, groups. Huddles before opening, run by Matt. The closing-time hand-off matters most: from 2 PM, every customer hears where to go next.

  • Week of Oct 26: 20-minute kickoff, with the Alpine team if possible, so both counters say the same thing about each other.
  • Dec 1, Jan 4, Feb 1, Mar 1: 10-minute monthly refresh: the order-by dates, the signs, the dates coming up.
  • When the hotel breakfast launches: what's served there, its hours, and how to answer "is that you?"
  • Every new hire: the card on day one.
