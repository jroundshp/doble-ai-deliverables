Cooperstown Clubhouse: two jobs while it's closed

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: the Clubhouse is closed until spring 2027, but people still find it on Google, follow it on Facebook and call it. All winter it does two jobs. First, it sends everyone who finds it to Fenimore's, for food, football, holiday parties and events. Second, it books groups and teams for summer 2027, starting in January, when coaches are planning their Cooperstown week.

01Where it stands today

Read from the live website and the work done so far, 1 October 2026.

"Closed for the season. See you in spring 2027." Buttons go to Fenimore's for food, online orders and team dinners. Fenimore's hours and phone are in the footer.

Answering for the Clubhouse

The short answer

"The Clubhouse is closed for winter and reopens in spring 2027. We're open right here every day, and we do team dinners too."

Team or group for summer 2027
  • Team name, coach's name, phone or email
  • Dates in Cooperstown, how many adults and kids
  • Send them to the Teams page, or hand the details to Bob the same day
Don't promise
A price that isn't on the Teams page, a walk-in for a big group, or a reopening date before it's announced.

07How we'll know it's working

  • Summer 2027 team inquiries per month, from January (the form, once it's counted in Google Analytics).
  • Team parties booked and deposits taken.
  • Clicks from the Clubhouse site to Fenimore's (Google Analytics).
  • Reach of the shared posts on Facebook.
