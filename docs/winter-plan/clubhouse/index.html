Cooperstown Clubhouse: two jobs while it's closed

Draft prepared 1 October 2026 by Doble AI for Corey Hogan. One of five property plans; the overview shows how they fit together.

Objective: the Clubhouse is closed until spring 2027, but people still find it on Google, follow it on Facebook and call it. All winter it does two jobs. First, it sends everyone who finds it to Fenimore's, for food, football, holiday parties and events. Second, it books groups and teams for summer 2027. Both start on November 1. Anyone who wants a party, a team dinner or an event this winter is sent to Fenimore's, which is booking now. Anyone planning summer 2027 goes to the Clubhouse Teams page for the details and an inquiry form, as agreed with Bob on 1 October. The summer push builds in January, when coaches plan their Cooperstown week.

Answering for the Clubhouse

The short answer

"The Clubhouse is closed for winter and reopens in spring 2027. We're open right here, and we're booking parties, team dinners and events for this winter now. For summer 2027 at the Clubhouse, I can take your details or you can send an inquiry on its Teams page."

Team or group for summer 2027
  • Team name, coach's name, phone or email
  • Dates in Cooperstown, how many adults and kids
  • Send them to the Teams page, or hand the details to Bob the same day
Don't promise
A price that isn't on the Teams page, a walk-in for a big group, or a reopening date before it's announced.

09How we'll know it's working

  • Summer 2027 team inquiries per month, from November (the form, counted in Google Analytics).
  • Team parties booked and deposits taken.
  • Clicks from the Clubhouse site to Fenimore's (Google Analytics).
  • Reach of the shared posts on Facebook.
