05 Priority Recommendations
01 Claim the Google Profile and Answer the Reviews
Do this before anything else in this document. The profile is unclaimed, so nobody can reply, correct the phone number, add photos or post availability. Claim it through Google's verification, then respond to the one-star reviews the way a professional operator would: acknowledge, state what was done or is being done, and offer a direct contact. A reply does not erase a complaint, but an answered complaint reads completely differently from an unanswered one — and right now it has stood for two years with silence next to it.
02 Run the AI Test Yourself — Ten Minutes
Open ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and a plain Google search . Set each one up properly, because "use a private window" is only correct for two of the four. For Google and Perplexity, an incognito window is the right control, though on Google it will not change your location, which it reads from your internet connection. ChatGPT needs Temporary Chat instead: its Memory is attached to your account rather than your browser and is switched on by default, so a private window does not touch it. Grok requires a sign-in, so incognito only gives you a login screen; sign in and start a brand new conversation rather than continuing an existing thread. If you can, have a few different people run the same set, ideally five, since these systems are not deterministic and one person running it once is a sample of one. Then type what a renter would type: "affordable apartments for rent in Buffalo NY" · "who are the best landlords or property managers in Niagara County" · "pet friendly affordable rentals in Erie County New York" · "do I need good credit to rent an apartment in Buffalo" · and "what do you know about Homestead Realty WNY in Getzville New York". Note who gets named and whether Homestead appears at all. That last prompt is the revealing one. Everything in this audit is an explanation of what you will see.
03 Start a Review Habit With Long-Term Tenants
Four reviews is a number a single good month reverses, and after nearly forty years there are tenants who have renewed repeatedly and would say so. Ask at renewal, after a maintenance job closes well, and at move-in. A dozen genuine reviews takes the average from 3.0 to comfortably above 4.0 and pushes the two one-stars down the page where a first-time visitor may never reach them. This is the fastest and cheapest reputational repair available.
04 Fix the Empty Title Tag and the Missing Descriptions
The homepage title tag is literally empty, which is the most damaging single on-page defect a site can carry — it is the headline Google shows in results and the strongest ranking signal on the page. Give every page a real title in the pattern "Affordable Rentals in Buffalo & Niagara County | Homestead Realty WNY," add a meta description to all twelve, and replace bare fragments like "Payments" with something a searcher would click.
05 Publish the Full Portfolio, Not Three Units
Three published units against a claimed sixty means most renters who arrive find nothing and leave. Publish every available unit with photos, rent, bedrooms, neighbourhood and availability date, and keep them current. Each becomes a page that can rank for its own street and area — "apartments for rent Oliver Street North Tonawanda" is a query with real intent and almost no competition.
06 List on Zillow Rentals and Apartments.com
This is where renters actually start, and Homestead is not there. The portals carry the search traffic, the filters renters use, and syndication into dozens of other sites. For a rental business this outranks the website in importance, and getting listed is largely free. It also creates the third-party citations that both Google and AI assistants use to corroborate a business exists.
07 Clean Up the Unfinished WordPress
Delete the "Hello world!" starter post and the uncategorized archive — both are live and in the public sitemap, and both signal an abandoned site. Remove or noindex /author/admin/, which is publishing that the administrator account is named "admin," and rename that account: it is the first username every automated WordPress attack tries. Hide the WordPress version string while you are in there.
08 Add Schema for the Business and Every Unit
The site has no structured data at all. Add RealEstateAgent or LocalBusiness markup with the Getzville address, the correct phone and service areas, then Apartment or Residence markup on each unit with rent, bedrooms, bathrooms and availability. This is what lets Google and AI assistants read the portfolio as facts rather than prose, and it is the layer that makes listings eligible to be quoted in an answer.
09 Write the FAQ Renters Actually Search
Income and credit requirements, security deposits, application fees, pets, whether Section 8 or housing vouchers are accepted, how maintenance requests work, typical response times. These are high-intent, high-volume questions with almost no good local answers published anywhere. Answering them honestly does three jobs at once: it wins the search, it feeds AI answers, and it pre-screens applicants so fewer unsuitable enquiries reach the office.
10 Add an AI Assistant for Tenant Enquiries
Once the FAQ and unit data exist, an AI assistant on the site can answer availability, pricing, pet and application questions around the clock and route serious applicants to the office. Rental enquiries arrive at all hours and from people comparing several places at once; being the one that answers immediately wins a disproportionate share. It also absorbs the repetitive questions a small team currently fields by phone. This is Doble AI's signature layer.
