How this score works

Only the website countsEvery point is something the people running the site can change. Google reviews and ratings are not scored.
100 points, four groupsIs the information the same everywhere? Does everything work? Can people find it? Does the site tell the whole story?
A 10 is reachableEach missing point is named with its fix. Finish the list and the site scores 10.

Same scale as the restaurant scorecards. A product site is judged on what a buyer needs, so the Complete group asks about specs, data sheets and project details instead of menus and hours.

Sitemap and one addressThis broke in the last day. The sitemap is the list of pages the site hands Google and Bing, and robots.txt tells them to read it at https://interactivewaterparks.com/sitemap_index.xml. At 2:15 pm Mountain time on 22 September that address loaded all 56 pages. On 23 September it returns "404 Not Found", and so does every sitemap address ending in .xml. The 404 comes from the web server (nginx) before the request reaches WordPress: Yoast still builds the sitemap, and it loads at the same address with a slash added on the end. We checked it several times, requesting it the way a browser does and the way Googlebot does. Google and Bing will keep the pages they already know for now, but they can't pick up new or changed pages from the sitemap until it is back. The rest of this row is fine: no page is hidden from search, every page names itself as the original, and www and http both forward to https://interactivewaterparks.com.
Ask Digital Silk or the hosting company what changed in the server settings since 22 September, and to let .xml addresses reach WordPress again. When https://interactivewaterparks.com/sitemap_index.xml loads in a browser, it is fixed (3 points).
