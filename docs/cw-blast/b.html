Seven weeks left: the close-out list

Same theme as option A, seven weeks left before the season closes, told a different way. A shows the calendar. This one shows the work. A contractor wrapping up a site this fall has a short list left: pour the pad, put down base, cover the ground, get rid of the old concrete. Concrete Warrior can do every item on that list from one yard, so the email is laid out as that list.

It's built from what's already on the website: the materials, the removal service, the yard hours, and the four things a quote needs. It leaves prices off and asks people to call, the same as the site does, so it doesn't have to wait on a price check.
