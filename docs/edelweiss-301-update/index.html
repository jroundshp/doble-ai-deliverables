The Four Seasons is the surprise

These are highly sought after, and the Four Seasons loyalists must not see the value at $7,000,000 despite the large 1,573 square foot size and the prestigious name.

One must ask why, with so little 2-bedroom competition in Vail Village, Edelweiss 301 has not even received one offer. Why has Lodge Apt. Condo #262 also not received one offer? We can only conclude that right now, in this market, the buyers are not seeing value in these properties.

03

What do I need to do to sell my property now?

We have to increase the sense of value. There are only two ways to do that.

  1. Physically enhance the property

  2. Lower its acquisition price

Or take it off the market for a while

If you think you might want to come in and ski this winter and use the property, then taking it off the market for a while may be a discussion point.
