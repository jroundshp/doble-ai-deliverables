# Byrne & Jones Repair Pack

Two files, both generated by crawling every URL in your sitemap on August 16, 2026 and
building the internal link graph from the served HTML. Nothing here was written or
guessed. Every row points at a link that exists on your site right now.

## bj-link-repairs.csv (32 rows)

Broken, legacy and redirecting internal links, with the page each one sits on and the
target it should point to instead.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `type` | `broken_or_legacy_link` (a 404, a chain, or a retired URL) or `redirect_link` (trailing slash omitted) |
| `issue` | what happens when someone clicks it today |
| `on_page` | the live page carrying the link |
| `current_href` | the href as written in your HTML |
| `change_to` | the current canonical destination |

Start with row 2, the 404. On `/stlouis/asphalt-paving/ev-charging-station-installation/`
the button reading **"Contact for Free Project Evaluation"** points to
`/asphalt-paving/ev-charging-station-installation/#EVContact`, an old path that now
returns a 404. The anchor itself is correct and `id="EVContact"` is on the page, along
with a working Divi quote form. Changing the href to `#EVContact` fixes it.

The `redirect_link` rows are lower priority and mostly cosmetic. They cost a 301 hop per
click rather than breaking anything.

## bj-orphan-map.csv (28 rows)

Pages in your sitemap with zero inbound internal links. A crawler can reach them only
through the sitemap, and a visitor cannot reach them at all by clicking.

The `suggested_link_from` column names pages that already exist and already cover the
topic. These are suggestions from site structure, not instructions. Blank rows are ones
where the right home is a judgment call about your sales process rather than a structural
question, and those are yours to make.

Worth noting which pages are on this list: all four sports case studies, the cooperative
purchasing pages, `/in-the-press/`, `/sports/maintenance-request/` and `/project-tour/`.
These are sales assets, not filler.
