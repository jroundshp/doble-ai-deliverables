01 Business Snapshot
What It Is
A custom fiberglass (FRP) fabricator, not a distributor. Standard and fully custom equipment shelters and enclosures under the Enduro line, plus composite manhole covers, ductwork, Parshall flumes for flow measurement, FRP fans, scrubber systems, chemical storage buildings and booster-pump skid packages. An on-staff design team builds to spec at effectively any size.
Locations & Contact
Medina — 10478 Ridge Road (headquarters), (585) 735-9668. Lockport — 222 S Niagara St production facility. Amherst — 2410 North Forest Road. A second number, (585) 735-9965, also appears site-wide. Three facilities is a real competitive asset that the digital footprint currently under-represents.
Who Actually Buys
Municipal water and wastewater authorities, chemical and industrial plants, and the consulting engineers who write the specifications. These are considered, specification-driven purchases with long lead times — which is exactly why being named in a research answer matters more here than in retail. The buyer is researching long before anyone picks up a phone.
Content Assets
Genuinely strong and unusual for this sector: a 1,600-word material guide on fiberglass, a Fiberglass vs. Concrete comparison, Manhole Systems 101, guides to ductwork, flumes and shelters, plus a real case-study library with named projects. The expensive part — writing authoritative technical content — is already done. The cheap part, marking it up so machines can quote it, has not been.
Product Catalog
A separate catalog runs at products.vpcfiberglass.com, linked from the homepage more than fifty times. It is ThomasNet-hosted and carries zero JSON-LD. Every product in it is invisible as structured data to both Google Shopping-style surfaces and AI assistants, which is the single largest untapped asset here.
Why Now
B2B technical buying is where AI search has moved fastest, because the questions are specific and the answers are researchable. An engineer asking which manufacturer makes a corrosion-resistant enclosure for a chlorine room now gets a shortlist, not a page of links. VPC has the products, the plants and the technical writing to be on that shortlist and currently is not. The competitors being named are not better fabricators — they are better marked up.

05 Priority Recommendations
01 Put Product Schema on the Catalog
The catalog at products.vpcfiberglass.com carries zero structured data while being linked from the homepage more than fifty times. Add Product markup to every item — name, description, material, dimensions, application, manufacturer — so each becomes a citable entity rather than an unreadable page. This is the single highest-leverage item on the audit, and it does not require writing anything new.
02 Turn the Resource Library Into Marked-Up FAQs
The material guide, the concrete comparison and the 101 explainers already answer what buyers ask — in prose. Restructure the answers as explicit questions ("How long does an FRP enclosure last in a chlorine environment?", "Fiberglass or concrete for a wet well?", "What lead time for a custom shelter?") and wrap them in FAQPage schema. FAQ markup is the primary driver of AI citation, and VPC currently has none anywhere.
03 Clean the Indexed Clutter
Delete or noindex the live /locations/test-location/ page. Consolidate the four near-duplicate Enduro enclosure pages into one canonical product page and redirect the rest, recovering the relevance currently split across copies. Noindex the /author/ archive, which is publishing the web vendor's admin username to search engines. Add the missing meta description to /locations/.
04 Complete the Three-Location Local Footprint
Create a Google Business Profile for the Amherst facility at 2410 North Forest Road, which has none. Reconcile the Lockport profile's (888) 290-9522 against the numbers published on the site so they agree. Standardise "S Niagara" versus "South Niagara" site-wide, and add LocalBusiness markup per location. Three plants beats a competitor shipping from South Carolina — but only if the three are findable.
05 Ask a Decade of Customers for Reviews
Three reviews, one of them a delivery driver's coordinates, is not a reputation — it is an absence. Municipal engineers, plant managers and contractors who have bought from VPC for years will give a review if asked, and B2B review counts are so low across this category that even a dozen genuine ones would stand out. Reviews also feed directly into how AI assistants judge which suppliers to name.
06 Publish Spec-Grade Comparison Content
The Fiberglass vs. Concrete page proves the format works. Extend it to the comparisons specifiers actually run — FRP versus stainless, versus aluminium, versus precast — plus corrosion-resistance tables, load ratings and the BABA compliance angle already touched on. This is the material engines quote when asked which supplier fits a requirement, and almost none of VPC's competitors have published it either.
07 Reduce Dependence on the ThomasNet Channel
ThomasNet delivers qualified traffic, but it also lists 611 competing FRP shelter suppliers, ranks its own category pages above individual manufacturers, and currently hosts VPC's catalog on a subdomain it controls. Keep the channel, and build the owned equivalent alongside it so that a buyer researching the category can find VPC directly rather than only through a directory that monetises the alternatives.
08 Add an AI Spec Assistant to the Site
Add an AI assistant trained on the product catalog, material specs and case studies that can answer sizing, material-selection and lead-time questions and route a qualified enquiry to the design team. Specification research happens at all hours and long before contact; capturing it is worth more per enquiry here than in any consumer business in the portfolio, because a single custom shelter order dwarfs a restaurant ticket. This is Doble AI's signature layer.
