The pattern in both blocks

The discount is doing its hardest work on the nights that never needed help. Christmas week and Presidents' week sell out whether or not you discount them. A flat percentage cannot tell the difference between a night you have to fight for and a night the calendar hands you, so it cuts both by the same amount. Between these two stays alone, that is $3,487 to $3,934 on eighteen nights that carry no real occupancy risk.

04

Whether to take the block comes down to one number

Work backwards from the guaranteed number. Sell every Peak and High night, which is the easy 34, and you are still $10,050 short of the block. Closing that gap takes 35 of the remaining 51 soft nights.

Break-even occupancy
81%

Above roughly 81% occupancy across the winter, pricing to demand wins. Below it, take the 85-night booking and be glad of it.

If you have run 80%+ before

Turn the season-long discount off and price to the calendar. You will clear more, and you keep the flexibility to take a high-value short stay over Fall Festival or Mardi Gras.

If you have not, or you do not know

The block is a sound deal. Take it, but not at 25%, and not with the peak weeks priced flat inside it. Section 05 is how you fix that without losing the booking.

Nobody should be guessing at this. Your own booking history from last winter answers it in about ten minutes, and it is the first thing we would pull.

05

Four fixes, in order of how much they are worth

  • 1Gross up the peak nights so the discount lands where you meant it toAirbnb applies the length-of-stay percentage to whatever nightly rate is showing. So set the marquee nights high enough that the discount brings them down to your target, instead of below it.
  • 2Put a floor under the marquee windowsChristmas week, MLK, Presidents' week and Mardi Gras get a rate floor and a three-night minimum. A long stay is welcome to include them. It is not welcome to discount them.
  • 3Stop treating the weekly and the monthly as the same leverYour 10% weekly is a healthy incentive and it is not what is hurting you. The 20% to 25% monthly is a different animal, because it is the one that reaches across whole holiday weeks. Leave the weekly alone and bring the monthly down to 10–12%.
  • 4Close the soft nights with the café, not the rateThe 36 midweek nights are where a real concession belongs, and the welcome card costs you food cost instead of room revenue. A 25% cut on this block is $9,734 handed over in cash. The same guest would take breakfast for a small fraction of it.

The short version

Discount the nights that need it. Never the nights that sell themselves.

A single percentage applied across a whole winter cannot tell those apart, and in Ellicottville the gap between them is more than $380 a night. Set the calendar first, then let the discount work on what is left.
