VPC in 2027 and 2028

How to get the best year VPC can still make out of 2027, and how to set 2028 up as the record year at roughly thirty percent above baseline.

Built from VPC's own profit and loss exports through 9 September 2026, the division income accounts, public funding documents for New York, Texas and Florida, and the work already running with Larry on the closest job index. Backlog, capacity and ship dates are not in anything we hold, so every one of those is marked as a question for the books rather than answered here. Companion detail: closest job plan, digital options, findability review, website work order, digital design brief.

00  The short version

2027 is won by shipping what is already sold and getting named in specs that pay later. 2028 is the year to aim the thirty percent at, because it harvests design stage work being decided right now. A record year needs backlog and shop capacity behind it, and both of those numbers live in VPC's books rather than in this document.

 

The two goals, stated plainly

2027  ·  Stretch

Best year VPC can still make

Almost all of 2027's revenue comes from work already won, plus late cycle wins that are short enough to fabricate and ship inside the year. Aquatic work sold through this coming winter and spring can still open in summer 2027. Municipal FRP that broke ground in September 2026 is not an open sales window. It is a job someone else got specified into eighteen months ago.

So the 2027 job is to maximize shippable dollars out of the existing book and stop pretending the greenfield press releases are leads.

2028  ·  Home run

Roughly thirty percent up on baseline

2028 is the first year that can be built rather than harvested. The designs being drawn in late 2026 and 2027 bid in 2027 and build in 2028. Getting VPC named in those is a 2026 and 2027 activity with a 2028 payout.

That makes 2028 the high confidence record attempt, and it makes the next six months the part that decides it.

What thirty percent actually equals

Pick the baseline first. The number changes the whole plan, and only you can set it.

If the baseline isIt is2028 target at +30%

2026 is running ahead of 2025. January through August booked $7.88M against $6.32M for the same eight months of 2025, up 24.7 percent. Whether that clears the 2023 peak depends entirely on how September through December land, and the last two years split the calendar too differently to guess from: the first eight months were 66.7 percent of 2024 and 52.8 percent of 2025. There is no honest projection to make from a fourteen point spread. The books will settle it in January, and if 2026 beats $13.43M then the 2028 target moves up with it.

All three figures above are total income from VPC's profit and loss, not the sales by customer report. The two disagree year by year, most sharply in 2023, and the bridge between them has not been explained yet. Whichever one you pick has to be the one you measure against in 2028 as well.

Thirty percent of what, though

The target is written in sales dollars, and it is worth saying out loud that sales dollars are not the only way to call a record year. Gross margin moved from 45.7 percent in 2024 to 49.9 percent in 2025, and net income through the first part of 2026 had already passed the full year figure for both 2024 and 2025. On that measure VPC may be having its best year right now, while the revenue line is still undecided.

Which matters, because a thirty percent revenue year won on thinner work can be a worse year than a fifteen percent one. So set two numbers in the same session, not one: the sales target, and the margin floor it is not allowed to fall through to get there. If the honest answer is that the home run should be measured in profit rather than revenue, the whole plan still works. It just changes which jobs are worth chasing.

The honest verdict. 2028 is the record year to plan for. Calling 2027 the record attempt only makes sense if firm backlog already covers most of the way past $13.43M and the shop has the hours to ship it. Nobody has put those two numbers side by side yet. Do that, then decide on 15 October 2026: 2027 as the stretch, or 2028 as the primary.

EasyRec is the only one of the three that can still move next year's number, because it sells and ships inside the same season.

The funding clock sits on top of all three

Federal water appropriations fall hard next year. Bluefield Research puts FY2026 at $23.4 billion and FY2027 at an estimated $8.6 billion, a drop of about 63 percent, as the infrastructure act supplemental and the pandemic relief money run out. Two things matter more than the headline:

  • $26.8 billion of infrastructure act revolving fund money is still converting into project agreements and will keep converting into the early 2030s. Of $40.7 billion allotted, only $13.9 billion has reached project level agreements. The money is committed. It just has not turned into jobs yet.
  • Pandemic relief money is the only hard stop. Unspent funds revert to the Treasury on 31 December 2026, which is what produces the rush of small municipal projects going out this fall.

Chase what is in design, on a funding list, or about to bid for 2027 and 2028 construction. A groundbreaking photograph is a job VPC either won or lost eighteen months ago.

02

2027: the best year still available

The first move takes about a week, and everything else on this page is sized off it.

This week: get the truth out of the books

Six numbers. None of them are in anything Doble AI holds, and the plan cannot be sized without them.

  1. Backlog by ship month, through the end of 2028. Signed orders only, no quotes. This is the single number that decides whether 2027 is a record attempt.
  2. How much of that backlog is InterActive Play. That division has been 44 percent of six year sales and it is the most volatile line in the company.
  3. Shop capacity, written down. Fabrication hours available per month by product family, against the hours the backlog already consumes. A record year VPC cannot build is a forecast, not a plan.
  4. On time ship percentage for the last twelve months. If it is poor, capacity is not the constraint, scheduling is, and the fix is cheaper.
  5. The revenue recognition rule, in one sentence. The customer report and the profit and loss disagree year by year, and 2023 is the extreme case: $10.21M on one and $13.43M on the other. Until someone says which one a 2028 target is measured on, the target is not defined.
  6. Third party revenue split out from intercompany. VPC bills work to other companies you own, and 2025 carried more of it than 2024 did. Growing total revenue by thirty percent and growing outside revenue by thirty percent are two different jobs with two different sales plans. Ava in finance can split it in an afternoon.
  7. Revenue by product family inside Traditional FRP. Flumes and metering manholes against shelters, enclosures and custom buildings. Nothing we hold splits them, and the answer decides whether section 04's Traditional FRP play is aimed at the right half of the division.

Q4 2026: lock what 2027 can ship

  • Lock the shippable dollars Take the backlog file and mark every line as shippable in 2027, shippable in 2028, or at risk. The 2027 revenue plan is the first column plus whatever short cycle work can still be sold.
  • Kill the dead quotes Every open quote older than forty five days gets chased to a yes or a no, or closed. Forty five rather than ninety, because a stretch year cannot carry a log nobody has pruned: it makes the pipeline look twice its real size and it buries the jobs worth calling. Municipal FRP quotes genuinely waiting on a design milestone get re-dated with that milestone written on them rather than closed, so the slow jobs do not go out with the dead ones.
  • Write the capacity envelope One page, signed by production, saying what the shop can ship per month in 2027. Sales sells against that number rather than around it.
  • Engineer sprint Top twenty five consulting engineers of record by volume in New York, Texas and Florida. A named owner, a call, a lunch and learn booked, a current guide specification in their hands.
  • Build America packet One document per product family covering domestic content and compliance, ready to attach the moment a revolving fund job appears. A missing packet is a disqualification, not a negotiation.
  • Mid construction packages Jobs already under construction still buy flumes, manholes and enclosures late. Those are real 2027 purchase orders and they are the only part of the municipal lane that can still move next year.

The 2027 program

InterActive Play. Two lists, kept separate. One is what is still buyable for a summer 2027 opening, which means it has to be sold and released to fabrication by roughly March. The other is designs being drawn now for 2028 openings, where the job is to get named in the drawing rather than to quote. Do not let the second list get scored as pipeline for next year.

EasyRec. Attach it to every aquatic quote that goes out, without exception. Benches, ADA stairs and accessories are short cycle, they ship inside the same year they are sold, and they travel to a buyer who is already talking to VPC. At 4.9 percent of six year sales it is the smallest lane and the easiest one to double, which is worth doing and is not a plan on its own. The mix rule in section 03 is what keeps it in proportion.

Make the quote promise real. The contact page already commits to it in your own name: all price quotations are responded to within 24 hours of request so long as all necessary specifications are provided. Measure it. If the real median is three days, either the promise comes down or the process comes up, and the closest job work in section 06 is the cheapest way to bring the process up.

The scoreboard for 2027

Four numbers on one page, reviewed monthly: on time ship percentage, quote turnaround against the twenty four hour promise, backlog against the monthly ship target, and revenue mix by division. Nothing else. A dashboard with twenty metrics gets read once.

03

2028: the home run

A thirty percent year is not a harder version of this year. It is a different year, and it is mostly decided before it starts.

What has to be true by 31 December 2027

  • Backlog heavy entry VPC walks into January 2028 with a large majority of the target already signed. If 2028 opens needing to sell most of its own year, it will not be a record year.
  • Spec position banked A countable number of 2028 construction jobs where VPC is named or basis of design in the engineer's drawings, tracked as a list with names on it rather than as a feeling.
  • Capacity proven, not assumed Either the shop demonstrated it can ship the monthly rate during 2027, or the added capacity is bought and installed during 2027. Not both decided in January.
  • Quote turnaround fixed The twenty four hour promise being met on standard products, so the extra volume does not arrive and sit.

How the year runs

QuarterThe job
Q1 2028Release aquatic work to fabrication for summer openings. Close the winter bid season on municipal FRP. Confirm the capacity plan against the real order book rather than the December forecast.
Q2 2028Ship the aquatic season. Protect the schedule, because a late splash pad is a lost reference for 2029. Keep the engineer program running through the quarter when everyone is busiest, because that is what fills 2029.
Q3 2028Municipal FRP shipping weight. Attack the fall revolving fund and bond cycle. This is the quarter that decides whether the year lands on target or a little under.
Q4 2028Close out, then repeat the same October decision for 2029 and 2030. The plan is a loop, not a one year push.

The mix rule

Traditional FRP and EasyRec fund the floor. They are steadier, they ship in shorter cycles, and they are what keeps a bad aquatic year from becoming a bad company year. InterActive Play buys the upside, and it is the only division that can add several million dollars on a handful of contracts, but it cannot be the only path to the number. It has been 44 percent of six year sales and it has also been the line that swings hardest.

Write the 2028 target as three division numbers that add up, not one company number. If the plan only works when InterActive Play has a great year, it is a bet rather than a plan.

Where to find the work, by type

  • Engineer lunch and learns. The consulting engineer writes the specification eighteen months before the purchase order. It is the best hour anyone at VPC spends.
  • Utility project managers. The owner side, who can tell you what is in design before it is public and who can ask the engineer for a product by name.
  • Construction managers before the guaranteed maximum price. There is a window on every construction manager at risk job where the product is still open. After the price is set it closes.
  • Parks capital plans and bond measures. Aquatic money is voted on in public and appears in a capital improvement plan long before it appears on a bid board.
  • Bid boards, filtered. BidNet and city portals, watched on product keywords rather than browsed: flume, metering manhole, enclosure, splash pad, ADA stair, pool lift.

06

How the AI account accelerates sales

The plan above adds work: more engineers to touch, more funding documents to read, more quotes to turn around faster. VPC has 57 people and none of them are sitting idle. The corporate account is how the extra work gets absorbed without hiring against every line of it.

The shape is already agreed in the closest job plan: one VPC owned corporate AI account, Grok Bot, with Corey as administrator, seats issued and revoked by VPC, and the records staying on VPC's own drives and in Salesforce. The bot is the front door. It is not the filing cabinet. Every workflow below runs against data VPC already owns, so swapping the front door later costs nothing.

Lane by lane

  • 01  Quote speed The first workflow, already under way with Larry. Index the metering manhole and flume submittal history, rank the closest past jobs against a new quote, flag which fields carry over and which have to change, and flag where the sales order, the submittal and the purchase order disagree. Five to ten submittals a week at three to five hours each, with most of the content already sitting in a job finished months ago. The hours go into finding it. Larry takes the first seat.
  • 02  Design stage watch Read the New York, Texas and Florida funding documents every month, diff them, and surface only the new funded and design stage rows. Draft the first outreach email to the engineer or utility named on each one. Keep the stage tag current as a row moves from design to funded to bid to construction. This is the section 05 job, and it is the one a person will quietly stop doing in March.
  • 03  Bid board triage Watch BidNet and the city portals on VPC product keywords, then summarize only the ones that match something VPC actually makes. The value is the noise it removes, not the leads it finds.
  • 04  Specifications and compliance Keep the guide specification language in construction specification format, current and ready to send, plus a domestic content one pager per product family. When a revolving fund job appears, the right packet comes out in minutes instead of being rebuilt.
  • 05  Sales enablement One pagers by product family, competitor cut sheet comparisons, lunch and learn outlines for the engineer program, and outbound that reads like a person wrote it. Anything that reads like a template gets deleted by the same engineers VPC is trying to reach.
  • 06  Play and recreation Track bond votes, referendums and parks capital plans so the aquatic pipeline is built from public records rather than from RFPs. Summarize the waterpark trade coverage. On the EasyRec side, support the measuring guide and configurator work so reps and dealers submit clean, labeled orders instead of sketches that come back as rework.
  • 07  Owner dashboard One weekly page for Corey: backlog against the ship calendar, open quote age, new design stage rows, and any job at risk of shipping late. Four things, and nothing that exists only to look good on a slide.

Findability is the inbound half

Everything above is outbound. The other half is being found by the engineer who is already searching, and by the AI answers that engineers now use before they search. That means product and question markup on pages VPC owns rather than rents, which is the decision set out in digital options and the gap list in the findability review. The directory listing stays only if the analytics show it pays for itself.

Rules for this account

  • Seats and billing sit under VPC. Corey administers. Seats are added only when a workflow has earned one, starting at one seat for Larry.
  • Customer files never live only inside a vendor chat. The drive is the record. The bot reads it.
  • No unsupervised loops and nothing that commits VPC to anything. Every output is a draft a person sends.
  • It does not replace Larry and it does not replace a salesperson. It removes search time and triage time, which is what both of them are currently spending their hours on.
  • Custom buildings and shelters are out of scope for now, and that is deliberate. They are the largest share of the engineering work and the hardest to model, and nothing in the material we hold supports modeling them yet. Metering manholes and flumes first, then quote history, then the harder families.

On cost. The first workflow sits inside the current arrangement rather than as a new line item. Beyond it, the account is a per seat monthly subscription billed to VPC plus build time, and we are not putting a seat rate on this page, because the published figures and the figures on an invoice have a habit of disagreeing. We will confirm the current rate in writing before anything is signed.

What kills the home run

  • Concentration InterActive Play has been 44 percent of sales and it does not repeat. One large contract slipping a quarter takes the year with it. The mix rule in section 03 is the only defense.
  • The shop If fabrication cannot ship the monthly rate, every extra order becomes a late order and a damaged reference. Capacity is the one constraint that cannot be fixed in December.
  • A slower hangover than expected The revolving fund money committed under the infrastructure act is real, but it converts into projects on a schedule nobody at VPC controls. If it converts slower than assumed, 2028 municipal volume arrives in 2029.
  • The relief money scramble Everything racing to be obligated before 31 December 2026 makes this fall look busier than the underlying market is. Do not read a busy October as a trend.
  • Losing the specification Getting written out of a drawing at 90 percent design is silent. Nobody calls to tell you. It shows up as a lost bid eighteen months later.
  • Slow quotes The site promises 24 hours in Corey's name. An engineer who waits three days goes to the fabricator who answered.
  • A compliance miss One missing domestic content packet is a disqualification on a funded job, not a negotiation.
  • Declaring 2027 too early Calling 2027 the record year before the backlog file exists is the most likely way to spend 2027 chasing a number the shop was never able to ship, and to arrive at 2028 with no specification position built.
