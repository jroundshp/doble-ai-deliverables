Beyond the website

These live on other platforms, so they don't count toward the score. They are still worth doing.

To fix

  • Instagram catering link: the "Contact for Catering Inquiries" link in the bio uses an old address, /wholesale-inquiries, which takes two redirects before it lands on the catering page. Fix: in the Instagram profile, change the link to ellicottvillebakeshop.com/catering-and-special-orders/.

Worth a look

  • Google profile: 4.4 stars from 110 reviews, listed as a Bakery with the right address, phone and hours. Google marks the hours as "updated by others," meaning a member of the public last changed them. Confirming them from the owner's account makes them official.
  • Facebook: address, phone, email and links are all correct. The intro could mention the daily hours and the village location, as the Instagram bio does.
  • Old menu address: /ebs-menu, which still turns up in search results, takes two redirects to reach the Menu page. That redirect is set on the hosting server, not in WordPress, so it needs whoever manages the hosting account.
