The Core Finding: The Expensive Work Is Done, The Cheap Work Is Not

Most audits we run find a real business behind a broken website. Aureo is the opposite, and that is worth saying plainly before anything else: the build quality here is better than any site we have audited. Server-rendered content that AI crawlers can actually read, a complete and genuinely decoupled Spanish and English mirror across 176 URLs, correct hreflang with an x-default, canonical tags on every page with zero mismatches, and a content library of 43 bilingual posts, 20 newsletters, research reports and two calculators. Nothing in this audit recommends a rebuild.

The gap is one layer up, and it is sharper than "some missing markup." The FAQ sections on the commercial pages are Radix accordions whose answer panels stay unmounted until a visitor clicks. That is a reasonable engineering choice for a human audience. The side effect is that the answer text never reaches the server-rendered HTML. We checked every page: across the 16 commercial pages there are 114 answer regions and all 114 are empty in the server response. The answers are not merely unmarked, they are absent. A crawler that does not execute JavaScript, which is most of the ones that matter here, sees a stack of questions and no answers.

The homepage is the exception, and it is the proof: it ships a FAQPage block carrying all seven of its answers, which is why the homepage FAQ is machine-readable and the other fourteen pages are not. The correct pattern is already in the codebase. It was simply never rolled out. Separately, 47 blog posts contain written question-and-answer sections with no FAQPage markup either, though on those the answers at least reach the HTML. The writing was the hard and expensive part and it is finished. What is missing is a translation layer, and section 08 of this audit hands it over already built.

01 · Business Snapshot

Who They Are

Company
Aureo (aureobitcoin.com), formerly Swapido. A Bitcoin-only financial platform serving Mexico. Operating entities are Swapido S.A.S. de C.V. and Soluciones Tecnologicas Nagumatech S.A.P.I. de C.V.
What They Sell
Buying and selling Bitcoin against Mexican pesos over SPEI, delivered direct to the customer's own wallet on-chain or over Lightning. Three segments: individuals, businesses, and Patrimonio, a private OTC desk for high net worth clients and family offices. Bitcoin only. No altcoins, no stablecoins, no yield products.
Regulatory Posture
Registered in the Bitcoin Service Provider registry administered by the Central Reserve Bank of El Salvador and supervised by that country's Superintendency of the Financial System. Aureo Mexico is registered with the SAT as a Vulnerable Activity under LFPIORPI. This is stated clearly on the site, which is more than most of the category does.
Backing & Team
$1.1M pre-seed from Early Riders, announced October 2025. The founding team previously built Veriphi, a Bitcoin-only exchange in Canada acquired by Bull Bitcoin in 2021, then worked on Lightning and self-custody tooling at Bull Bitcoin before relocating to Mexico.
Site Stack
Next.js, server-rendered. Roughly 270 KB of gzipped JavaScript, sub-second time to first byte, HTTP/2. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are both installed. Performance is not a problem on this site and does not appear in the fix list.
Earned Media
Coverage in Bitcoin Magazine, news.bitcoin.com, cryptonews, and Newswire, plus a Stephan Livera Podcast appearance (episode 701). Real third-party validation already exists. It is not currently declared anywhere a machine can read it.
02 · Baseline

What Is Already Right

Audits are more useful when they are specific about what not to touch. Everything in this list was verified against the served HTML and should be left alone:

Verified Strengths
  • Genuinely decoupled bilingual content. The Spanish and English versions are separately written, not machine-translated, with correct lang attributes (es-MX and en) on all 176 pages. This is rare and it is expensive to produce.
  • Complete crawl infrastructure. robots.txt is correct, sitemap.xml carries full xhtml:link hreflang annotations including x-default, and every page has a self-referencing canonical. We checked all 176 and found zero canonical mismatches.
  • Content is server-rendered. A representative blog post delivers roughly 26,000 characters of readable text in the initial HTML response. AI crawlers, most of which do not execute JavaScript, can read the whole library.
  • The legacy URL migration was handled correctly. Old /editorial/ paths 301 to /blog/ with correct canonicals. We checked because a stale /editorial/ URL still appears in Google results. That is reindexing lag, not a defect.
  • The content itself is strong. The tax material in particular is winning searches today, which is the proof that this approach works when the machine-readable layer is present.
03 · One Finding Worth Its Own Section

Every FAQ Answer on Every Commercial Page Is Missing From the HTML

Reproduce This in Ten Seconds

Run these two commands. The first counts the accordion answer regions on the individuals page. The second looks for the answer to your own KYC question, "hasta 5 minutos."

curl -s https://www.aureobitcoin.com/es/personas | grep -c 'role="region"'
→ 4

curl -s https://www.aureobitcoin.com/es/personas | grep -c 'minutos'

That single gap is the entire thesis of this audit in one query.

06 · Channel-by-Channel

Where the Score Comes From

A

Site Build & Performance

Server-rendered Next.js, roughly 270 KB of gzipped JavaScript across 21 chunks, 27 KB of CSS, time to first byte around half a second over HTTP/2. Content arrives in the HTML, which matters more than raw speed here because most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. No action needed.

A

Bilingual Execution

A complete, separately authored Spanish and English mirror across all 176 URLs with correct per-locale language attributes and full hreflang including x-default. Most bilingual sites we audit are a translation plugin over an English original. This is the real thing, and in the Mexican market it is a durable advantage.

A-

Content Depth & Quality

43 bilingual posts, 20 newsletters, research reports, and two working calculators. The tax and custody material is substantive enough that search engines already reward it. Marked down only slightly: the 40 newsletter pages run thin at roughly 200 words with no headings and no article-level schema, so they add page count without adding much retrievable substance.

B+

Technical SEO Foundation

robots.txt, a properly annotated sitemap, canonical tags on every page with zero mismatches, and a correctly executed legacy URL migration. The deductions are all metadata hygiene rather than architecture, and they are listed below.

C

Metadata Hygiene

83 of 176 meta descriptions exceed the roughly 160 characters Google will display, and 56 of those exceed 200 because they appear to be automatic dumps of the first paragraph rather than written summaries. The longest runs 591 characters. 55 title tags exceed 60 characters and will truncate in results. 19 pages carry more than one H1. Six newsletter pages have no meta description at all, and one Spanish tax post renders its brand suffix twice: ...guía SAT | Aureo | Aureo. Individually trivial, collectively this is the difference between a search result that sells and one that trails off mid-sentence.

C+

URL Hygiene

21 English posts are served on Spanish slugs, so an English reader and an English-language crawler both see /en/blog/cuanto-tarda-compra-bitcoin. Two live URLs carry typos that are also sitting in the sitemap: ...pay-locas-in-mexico... (intended "locals") and ...selling-Bitcoin-in-Mexido (intended "Mexico"). Several slugs mix upper and lower case. These have earned links, so the fix is a 301 rather than a rename.

D+

Structured Data & GEO Readiness

The foundation is present: Organization and WebSite schema sitewide, BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList on all 86 posts, FinancialService on two pages. What is missing is the layer that drives AI citation. FAQPage appears on 8 of 176 pages against 54 pages of written Q&A. The 40 newsletter pages carry no article schema at all. This single grade is most of the gap between 7.4 and 9.

D

Entity Graph

The Organization schema's sameAs property lists exactly one profile, twitter.com/aureobitcoin, while the site footer links out to X, LinkedIn, Instagram and a YouTube channel, all live. There is no foundingDate, address, contactPoint or founder. No Person schema exists for any of the six named team members, all of whom have public professional profiles. Separately, BlogPosting.author is declared as a Person named "Aureo," which describes the company as an individual. For regulated financial content, author identity and credentials are among the strongest quality signals available, and right now none of them are declared.

F

Category Search Visibility

Not visible on the primary commercial query in either language. This is the single largest source of missed demand on the audit, and it is also the slowest to fix, because the incumbents have a decade of authority. It is won with the content engine that already exists, pointed at different queries.

F

Third-Party Citation Presence

Absent from every aggregator and comparison property the engines cite for this category, in both languages. Genuine earned media exists (Bitcoin Magazine, Stephan Livera, news.bitcoin.com) but none of it lives on the specific pages that get pulled into "best exchange in Mexico" answers. This is outreach work, not engineering work, and it is the fastest available lever on the category problem.

07 · The Fix List

Eight Fixes, In Order of Return

01Get the FAQ Answers Back Into the HTMLAlready Built for YouHighest Return
Do on the other fourteen commercial pages what the homepage already does: ship the answers as a FAQPage block next to the accordion. We have built this and it is yours, free, in section 08 below. 112 question and answer pairs across all 16 pages in both languages, taken from your own copy, plus a 20-line server component. Nothing else on this list returns as much for as little. The 47 blog posts with written Q&A are the natural second pass.
02Fix the Author and Entity LayerWeek 1
Change BlogPosting.author from a Person named "Aureo" to the actual human authors, with Person schema carrying their credentials, and expand Organization.sameAs to include the LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube profiles that already exist. Add foundingDate, contactPoint, and founder. For regulated financial content this is a direct trust signal, and a team that came out of Veriphi and Bull Bitcoin has credentials genuinely worth declaring.
03Get Listed on the Properties the Engines CiteFastest Category Lever
Roughly a dozen named sites decide the "best exchange in Mexico" answer, listed in section 05. Most accept submissions or run editorial review programs. The Spanish-language set (criptonoticias, criptotendencias, espaciocripto, comparalatam) matters most and is the least contested by international competitors. A Bitcoin-only, regulator-registered, direct-to-wallet platform is a genuinely differentiated entry on lists currently full of interchangeable exchanges.
04Write the Comparison Content the Category Is Won With
Bitso ranks for "mejor exchange" partly on its own comparison post. The honest version of that page is available to Aureo and not to a full-service exchange: a direct comparison of the Mexican options, including a clear statement of who is better served elsewhere. Same for the business treasury and CFDI question, which is currently unanswered by anyone and which Aureo can answer better than any US source. The queries Aureo is losing are the editorial calendar.
05Rewrite the Meta Descriptions and Long TitlesMechanical
83 descriptions over 160 characters, 56 of them raw paragraph dumps, and 55 titles over 60. Replace the automatic fallback with a written field per page, in both languages, and add a length check in the publishing flow so the problem does not return. Fix the duplicated brand suffix on the Spanish tax post while in there.
06Correct the Typo Slugs and English URL Paths
301 the two typo URLs (pay-locas-in-mexico, selling-Bitcoin-in-Mexido) to corrected slugs, normalize the mixed-case URLs, and give the 21 English posts currently on Spanish slugs English equivalents with redirects from the old paths. These pages have earned links, so redirect rather than rename, and update the sitemap after.
07Decide What the Newsletter Archive Is For
40 pages at roughly 200 words with no headings and no article schema. Two defensible answers: give them proper Article schema, headings and descriptions so they contribute, or consolidate the archive and stop publishing each issue as an indexable page. The current middle position adds URLs without adding retrievable substance. Six of them have no meta description at all.
08Measure AI Visibility Monthly
Run a fixed set of buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Grok, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, in both languages, and screenshot the answers. Grok earns its place here specifically: this audience lives on X. Track who gets recommended, who gets cited, and which third-party sources the engines are leaning on that month. Once the schema work in fix 01 lands, this is how you find out what it bought, and the tax content gives a clean baseline that is already winning.

Aureo has already done what almost nobody in this category does: published deep, accurate, genuinely bilingual answers to the questions its buyers ask, on a fast site that machines can read. That is years of compounding work and it cannot be shortcut. What is missing is the thin translation layer that lets an AI assistant recognize those answers as answers, and a presence on the dozen properties those assistants already trust. A 7.4 with this much real substance behind it has a shorter path to 9 than any site we have audited.
