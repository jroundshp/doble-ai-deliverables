VPC Digital Presence

Half the product website is rented. Here is what that costs, what the competition does instead, and three ways to go.

Based on a live review of VPC's sites, the ThomasNet profile, and competitor sites checked on 11 September 2026. Companion detail: work order, findability review, closest job plan.

The short version

The directory ranking looks fine on paper. Leads from it are unproven until RFQs and wins are counted. The rented product catalog is the decision in front of you.

01

What you have now

The footer of every page on the main site says it out loud already: Site created by Thomas Marketing Services and Powered by Navigator Platform. Anyone who scrolls to the bottom can read who owns what.

Search FRP Shelters and VPC comes back on page one, without paying for placement. Ranking on ThomasNet is not the same as value. Keep the listing only if it can show real RFQs and wins. If it cannot, it is optional advertising, not part of the foundation.

The rest of the estate. VPC is not only FRP shelters. Interactive Play, the water park side, and EasyRec are a significant part of the business, and those sites (interactivewaterparks.com, easyrecreation.com) already run on WordPress that VPC controls, separate from the rented Thomas catalog. So the decision is bigger than Navigator. Over the next three to five years VPC should own the full digital presence across traditional FRP and play, instead of leaving product truth split across vendors and sister domains with no single owned system.

The problem is not ThomasNet. The problem is that half the product website is rented.

03

Options for you

Option A

Stay as-is

Keep WordPress, Navigator and ThomasNet exactly as they are. Cheapest in the short term. Nothing to manage.

Trade-off Split ownership continues for the next three to five years. The best description of a VPC product keeps living on someone else's platform.

Option B

Patch the current site

Fix findability on the pages VPC already controls. Leave Navigator as the catalog. Work we can start now.

Trade-off Buys time and improves what is there. Does not fix ownership. The catalog stays rented.

Recommended Option C

Rebuild and own everything

A new VPC-owned site with product pages, SEO, GEO and schema in place on day one. The rebuild covers the traditional FRP product pages and the play brands under VPC-owned infrastructure, either one site or clear owned siblings. Bring the good Thomas catalog copy home onto VPC pages, with redirects so nothing is lost.

Result ThomasNet stays only if it produces leads. It is not the product website. VPC owns a digital asset it can move, sell or hand to anyone.

The ask. Keep ThomasNet only if it pays. Stop renting the product site. Own the full digital presence.
